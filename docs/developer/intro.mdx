# Mezon developer documentation

Build bots, channel apps and integrations for Mezon. Every app and bot starts in the [Mezon developer portal](https://mezon.ai/developers/applications), where you create it and get its credentials.

## Start here

1. [What you can build](./quick-start/overview.md): bots, channel apps, webhooks and OAuth2 in one page.
2. [Create a bot](./quick-start/creating-mezon-bot.md) or [create a channel app](./quick-start/creating-mezon-app.md) in the developer portal.
3. [Write your first bot](./mezon-sdk/integration-bot-sdk/getting-started.md) with the JavaScript/TypeScript SDK.

## Bots

A bot is a program that logs in to Mezon with its own account. It reads messages in the clans it was added to and answers them.

- [JavaScript/TypeScript bot SDK](./mezon-sdk/integration-bot-sdk/overview.md) (`mezon-sdk` on npm, Node.js)
- [.NET bot SDK](./mezon-sdk/dotnet-sdk.md) (`Mezon.Net.Sdk` on NuGet)
- [What bots are allowed to do](./mezon-sdk/bot-api-access.md)
- [Interactive messages](./mezon-topics/interactive-message.mdx): buttons, forms and selects
- [Stream channels](./mezon-topics/streaming-channel.mdx): publish video from a bot

## Channel apps

A channel app is your website, opened from a channel in a clan. Mezon tells your website who the user is with signed data.

- [Channel apps overview](./mezon-sdk/channel-app-sdk/overview.md)
- [Check who opened your app](./mezon-sdk/channel-app-sdk/channel-app-auth-flow.mdx)

## Webhooks

A webhook lets any program post to Mezon with one HTTP request. You need no bot and no SDK.

- [Channel webhook](./webhooks/channel-webhook.mdx): post a message to a channel.
- [Clan webhook](./webhooks/clan-webhook.mdx): send a message to one member's inbox.

## Log in with Mezon

- [OAuth2](./mezon-topics/oauth2.mdx): let people sign in to your website with their Mezon account.
