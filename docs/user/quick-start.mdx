# Get started with Mezon

**Start on your phone**: install Mezon, log in and join your team's clan. Then add your computer. You keep one account, and your clans are on every device.

**You need:**

- A phone with Android 7.0 or later, or an iPhone with iOS 13.0 or later.
- An email address you can open.
- Your team's clan invite link or QR code. Your team leader sends it, for example in a group chat.
- For the computer (optional): Windows 10 (version 1809) or later, macOS 13 or later, or 64-bit Linux (Ubuntu or Debian).

:::note
A **clan** is your team's space in Mezon. It has **channels**: text channels for messages and voice channels for talking.
:::

## 7. Use Mezon on your computer too

Do this after your phone is set up. Your clans and messages are already there.

**Install:** go to [mezon.ai](https://mezon.ai), scroll to the bottom and click **DESKTOP**. Click the button for your computer: **Windows**, **macOS** or **Linux Build**. Windows and macOS open the store; Linux downloads a `.deb` file that you open with your software installer.

<Image src={computerDownload} alt="The download buttons at the bottom of mezon.ai, with the DESKTOP menu open" width={460} />

Direct links: [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9pf25lf1fj17) · [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/mezon-desktop/id6756601798?mt=12). When a newer version exists, Mezon offers it inside the app. No install? Use Mezon in a web browser: go to [mezon.ai](https://mezon.ai) and click **Login** at the top.

**Log in with your phone (QR code):** the easiest way, and it keeps one account. The login screen on the computer shows a QR code.

<ShotSteps wide>
<Shot src={computerSignIn} alt="The web Sign In page with the QR code on the right">On your phone: **Profile** → gear icon → **Scan QR Code** (iPhone: **QR Scan**). Point the camera at the QR code on the computer. Tap **Log in**. Mezon opens on the computer with your clans and messages.</Shot>
<Shot src={computerLanguage} alt="The Language page in settings">The desktop app starts in English. To change it: gear icon next to your name (bottom left) → **App Settings** → **Language** → **Tiếng Việt**.</Shot>
</ShotSteps>

Or log in with the same email or phone number as on your phone, then type the 6-digit code that Mezon sends. Desktop app: it asks for a phone number first; to use your email, click **Login with Email OTP**, type it and click **Send OTP**. Web: type your email or phone number and click **Sign in**.

:::warning
Only scan a Mezon QR code that you see on your own computer. Never confirm a code that someone sent you.
:::
