# Get started with Mezon

This guide shows the first things to do in Mezon: install it, log in, join your team's clan, send a message, talk in a voice channel and invite other people.

In each part, choose **Phone** or **Computer**. Your choice is kept for the whole page.

## What you need

- A phone with Android 7.0 or later, or an iPhone with iOS 13.0 or later.
- Or a computer with Windows 10 (version 1809) or later, macOS 13 or later, or 64-bit Linux (Ubuntu or Debian).
- An email address you can open.
- The invite link to your team's clan, if someone sent you one.

:::note
A **clan** is your team's space in Mezon. A clan has **channels**: text channels for messages and voice channels for talking.
:::

## 1. Install Mezon

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

**Android phone**

1. Open **Google Play**.
2. Search for **Mezon**. The publisher is **NCCPLUS**.
3. Tap **Install**.

Or open this link on your phone: [Mezon on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mezon.mobile).

**iPhone**

1. Open the **App Store**.
2. Search for **Mezon**. The provider is **NCCPLUS VIETNAM JOINT STOCK COMPANY**.
3. Tap **Get**.

Or open this link on your iPhone: [Mezon on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/mezon/id6502750046).

:::tip
One link works on both kinds of phone: [mezon.ai/mobile-download](https://mezon.ai/mobile-download). It opens the right store for your phone. You can print it as a QR code for your team.
:::

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Go to [mezon.ai](https://mezon.ai) and scroll to the bottom of the page.
2. Click **DESKTOP**. One button appears for your computer: **Windows**, **macOS** or **Linux Build**.

<Image src={computerDownload} alt="The download buttons at the bottom of mezon.ai, with the DESKTOP menu open" />

3. Click that button. Windows and macOS open the store; Linux downloads a `.deb` file.
4. Install Mezon from the store.
5. On Linux, open the downloaded file with your software installer.

When a newer version exists, Mezon offers it inside the app.

Direct links: [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9pf25lf1fj17) · [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/mezon-desktop/id6756601798?mt=12)

:::tip
You can also use Mezon without installing it: go to [mezon.ai](https://mezon.ai) and click **Login** at the top.
:::

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## 2. Log in (this also creates your account)

You do not need to sign up first. The first time you log in, Mezon creates your account.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open Mezon and tap **Get Started**.

<Image src={phoneWelcome} alt="The Mezon welcome screen with the Get Started button" width={270} />

2. If you see a phone number field, tap **Login with Email OTP**.
3. Enter your email.

<Image src={phoneLoginEmail} alt="The log-in screen with the email field" width={270} />

4. Tap **Send OTP**. Mezon sends a 6-digit code (OTP) to your email.
5. Type the code and tap **Verify OTP**.
6. If Mezon asks for a Mezon name, type your name without accents or spaces, for example **nguyenvanan**. This becomes your username. Then tap **Update**.
7. When Mezon asks to send notifications, tap **Allow**. Then you see new messages.

<Image src={phoneAllowNotifications} alt="The notification permission question with the Allow button" width={270} />

Mezon opens. If you are not in a clan yet, go to part 3 to join one.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

**Desktop app**

1. Open Mezon. The login screen shows a phone number field.
2. Click **Login with Email OTP**.
3. Enter your email and click **Send OTP**.
4. Type the 6-digit code (OTP) from your email.
5. If Mezon asks for a Mezon name, type your name without accents or spaces, for example **nguyenvanan**. Then click **Update**.

**Web browser**

The login page is in English.

1. Enter your email.
2. Click **Sign in**.
3. Type the 6-digit code from your email.
4. Click **Verify OTP**.

<Image src={computerSignIn} alt="The Sign In page with the email or phone number field and a QR code" />

Mezon opens. If you are not in a clan yet, go to part 3 to join one.

**Log in with your phone instead (QR code)**

The login screen on the computer also shows a QR code. If you are already logged in on your phone:

1. On the phone, tap **Profile** at the bottom, then the gear icon.
2. Tap **Scan QR Code** (iPhone: **QR Scan**).
3. Point the camera at the QR code on the computer.
4. Tap **Log in**.

:::warning
Only scan a Mezon QR code that you see on your own computer. Never confirm a code that someone sent you.
:::

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

You can also log in with a Vietnamese mobile number on the apps. The code then comes by SMS.

### Change the language

The Android app and the desktop app start in English. To use Vietnamese:

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap **Profile** at the bottom, then the gear icon at the top.

<Image src={phoneProfileSettings} alt="The Profile tab with the gear icon marked" width={270} />

2. Tap **Language**.

<Image src={phoneSettingsLanguage} alt="Settings with the Language row marked" width={270} />

3. Tap **Tiếng Việt**.

<Image src={phoneLanguageChoice} alt="The language list with English and Tiếng Việt" width={270} />

On an iPhone, Mezon uses the phone's language. You can change it in the same place.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the gear icon next to your name, at the bottom left.
2. Under **App Settings**, click **Language**.
3. Click **Tiếng Việt**. The language changes at once.

<Image src={computerLanguage} alt="The Language page in settings" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## 3. Join a clan, create a clan, add a channel

### Join your team's clan

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the invite link you received (for example in an SMS, email or chat). It looks like `https://mezon.ai/invite/...`.
2. Mezon opens and shows the clan. Tap **Join**.

<Image src={phoneInviteOpen} alt="The invite screen with the clan name and the Join button" width={270} />

The clan opens. If you are not logged in yet, log in first. Mezon then shows the invite again.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the invite link you received. It looks like `https://mezon.ai/invite/...`.
2. If the page asks **Open this link in the Mezon desktop app?**, click **Open app** only if you installed the desktop app. Otherwise, close the question.
3. Click **Accept Invite**.

<Image src={computerJoinInvite} alt="The invite page with the Accept Invite button" />

You are now in the clan. Mezon shows its member list; click a channel on the left to start.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

### Create a clan (for the person who sets up the team)

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap **+** at the bottom of the clan bar on the left.

<Image src={phoneAddClan} alt="The plus button at the bottom of the clan bar" width={270} />

2. Tap **Create My Own**.

<Image src={phoneCreateClanChoice} alt="The Create Your Clan screen with Create My Own and templates" width={270} />

3. Type the clan name. You can add a picture with **Upload**.
4. Tap **Create Clan**.

<Image src={phoneCreateClanForm} alt="The clan name form with the Create Clan button" width={270} />

Your new clan opens. It already has a text channel called **general**.

<Image src={phoneNewClan} alt="A new clan with the general channel" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click **+** (**Create Clan**) in the clan bar on the far left.

<Image src={computerCreateClanButton} alt="The plus button in the clan bar" />

2. Click **Create My Own**.

<Image src={computerCreateClanChoose} alt="The Create Your Clan dialog with Create My Own" />

3. Type the clan name under **CLAN NAME**. You can add a picture with **Upload**.
4. Click **Create**.

<Image src={computerCreateClanName} alt="The Customize Your Clan dialog with a name typed" />

Your new clan opens. It already has a text channel called **general**.

<Image src={computerNewClan} alt="A new clan open on the general channel" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The clan name can have up to 64 characters. It must not be used by any other clan in Mezon; if it is, choose another name.

### Add a channel

Only the clan owner and people with a role that can manage channels can add channels.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open your clan.
2. Press and hold the category name, for example **PUBLIC CHANNELS**.
3. Tap **Create channel**.

<Image src={phoneCategoryMenu} alt="The menu after pressing and holding a category name, with Create channel" width={270} />

4. Type the channel name.
5. Choose **Text** for messages or **Voice** for talking.
6. Tap **Create** at the top right.

<Image src={phoneCreateChannel} alt="The Create Channel form with the name and channel types" width={270} />

The new channel appears in the list. Voice channels have a speaker icon.

<Image src={phoneChannelList} alt="The channel list with two text channels and one voice channel" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open your clan.
2. Point at the category name, for example **PUBLIC CHANNELS**, and click the **+** next to it.

<Image src={computerCreateChannelButton} alt="The plus button next to the category name" />

3. Choose **Text** for messages or **Voice** for talking.
4. Type the channel name.
5. Click **Create Channel**.

<Image src={computerCreateChannel} alt="The create channel dialog with the channel types and name" />

The new channel opens.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## 4. Chat and talk

### Send a message to one person

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open a channel and tap its name at the top.
2. In **Members**, tap the person.

<Image src={phoneMemberList} alt="The members list of a channel" width={270} />

3. Tap **Message**.

<Image src={phoneProfileMessage} alt="A member's profile with the Message button" width={270} />

4. Type your message in the box at the bottom.
5. Tap the send button on the right. It appears when you start typing.

<Image src={phoneComposerSend} alt="A message typed in the box, with the send button marked" width={270} />

The newest messages are at the bottom, just above the box where you type. Your conversations are in the **Messages** tab at the bottom.

<Image src={phoneDmChat} alt="A conversation between two people, with the message box at the bottom" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. In a channel, click the members icon at the top right.
2. Right-click the person and click **Message**.

<Image src={computerMemberMenu} alt="The member menu with Message" />

3. Type in the box at the bottom and press **Enter**.

The newest messages are at the bottom, just above the box where you type. Your conversations are under **DIRECT MESSAGES**: click the Mezon logo at the top left to see them.

<Image src={computerDirectMessage} alt="A conversation between two people" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

### Talk in a voice channel

Voice channels have a speaker icon in the channel list.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap the voice channel.
2. Tap **Join as Speaker**.

<Image src={phoneVoiceSheet} alt="The voice channel sheet with Join as Speaker" width={270} />

3. The first time, allow Mezon to use the microphone: tap **While using the app**.

<Image src={phoneAllowMicrophone} alt="The microphone permission question" width={270} />

4. Your microphone starts off. Tap the microphone button at the bottom to talk.
5. To leave, tap the red button.

<Image src={phoneInCall} alt="The voice channel screen with the buttons at the bottom" width={270} />

While you talk, each person has a tile with their name. A crossed-out microphone means that person is muted. The buttons at the bottom are, from left to right: camera, microphone, chat, share screen and the red button to leave.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the voice channel.
2. Click **Join as Speaker**.

<Image src={computerVoiceJoin} alt="The voice channel screen before joining, with Join as Speaker" />

If 20 or more people are inside, the button says **Join as Audience** and you can only listen. To talk, click the arrow next to it and choose **Join as Speaker**.

3. The first time, allow Mezon to use the microphone.
4. Your microphone starts off. Click the microphone button at the bottom to talk.
5. To leave, click the red button at the bottom (**Disconnect**; desktop app: **Leave**).

<Image src={computerVoiceCall} alt="A voice channel with two people and the buttons at the bottom" />

The person who is speaking has a green frame. The buttons at the bottom also turn on your camera and share your screen. Point at a button to see its name.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## 5. Invite people to your clan

Anyone in a clan can invite people.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan and tap the clan name at the top.
2. Tap **Invite**.

<Image src={phoneClanMenu} alt="The clan menu with Invite" width={270} />

3. Choose one:
   - **Share Invite**, to send the link with another app.
   - **Copy Link**, then paste the link in a message.
   - **QR Code**, to let people scan the code with their phone.
   - **Invite** next to a friend's name, to send the link to that friend in Mezon.

<Image src={phoneInviteSheet} alt="The invite sheet with Share Invite, Copy Link and QR Code" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the clan name at the top of the channel list.
2. Click **Invite People**.

<Image src={computerClanMenu} alt="The clan menu with Invite People" />

3. Click **Copy** (desktop app: **Copy Link**) and paste the link in a message. Or click **Invite** next to a friend's name.

<Image src={computerInviteLink} alt="The invite dialog with the link and the Copy button" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::warning
Anyone who has the link can join your clan. Share it only with your team. If a link does not work, open the invite again to get a new one.
:::
