# Sign up and log in

You do not need to sign up first. The first time you log in with a code, Mezon creates your account.

## Ways to log in

| Method | Phone app | Desktop app | Web app |
|---|---|---|---|
| Code sent by SMS | Yes, Vietnamese mobile numbers (+84) | Yes, Vietnamese mobile numbers (+84) | Yes |
| Code sent by email | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Email and password | Yes, after you [set a password](#set-a-password) | Yes, after you set a password | Yes, after you set a password |
| QR code scanned with your phone | No (the phone is the scanner) | Yes | Yes |

The code is a 6-digit one-time code (OTP).

## Log in with a code {#log-in-with-a-code}

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open Mezon and tap **Get Started**.
2. Enter your phone number. To use email instead, tap **Login with Email OTP** and enter your email. Outside Vietnam, the email screen may show first.

<Image src={phoneLogin} alt="The phone login screen with the phone number field marked" width={270} />

3. Tap **Send OTP**.
4. Type the 6-digit code from the SMS or the email, then tap **Verify OTP**.
5. If Mezon asks you to **Enter Mezon name**, type your name and tap **Update**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

**Desktop app**

1. Open Mezon. The **WELCOME BACK** screen shows a phone number field.
2. Enter your phone number. To use email instead, click **Login with Email OTP** and enter your email.
3. Click **Send OTP**.
4. Type the 6-digit code.
5. If Mezon asks you to **Enter Mezon name**, type your name and click **Update**.

**Web app**

1. Go to [mezon.ai](https://mezon.ai) and click **Login**.
2. Type your email or phone number and click **Sign in**.
3. Type the 6-digit code and click **Verify OTP**.

<Image src={webLogin} alt="The Sign In page of the web app with the email or phone number field marked" />

The web login page is in English only.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Mezon opens. If this was your first login, your account is ready.

:::tip
No code yet? Wait until the timer ends, then select **Resend OTP**. If you typed a wrong email or number, use the link to change it. Outside Vietnam, log in with your email.
:::

## Log in with a password {#log-in-with-a-password}

You can use a password only after you [set one](#set-a-password).

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. On the login screen, tap **Login with Email & Password**.
2. Enter your email and your password.
3. Tap **Log In**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

**Desktop app:** click **Login with Email & Password**, enter your email and password, then click **Log In**.

**Web app:** under **Or sign in with**, click **Password**. Enter your email and password, then click **Sign in**. If you forgot your password, click **Forgot Password?**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Set a password {#set-a-password}

Your account needs an email address before you can set a password. If you logged in with a phone number, Mezon first asks you to link an email (web and iPhone: **Link Email Required**). Link your email, then set the password.

The password needs at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap **Profile** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap the gear icon at the top right to open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Account**. On an iPhone, then tap **Account Management**.
4. Tap **Set Password**.
5. Type the password twice and save.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the gear icon next to your name, at the bottom left.
2. Click **Account**.
3. Next to the password, click **Set Password**.
4. Type the password twice and click **Save**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Log in on a computer with your phone (QR code) {#log-in-on-a-computer-with-your-phone-qr-code}

If you are logged in on your phone, you can log in on a computer without a code.

1. On the computer, open the login screen. The desktop app shows the QR code under **Sign in by QR code**. The web login page shows it under **Log in with QR Code**.
2. On the phone, tap **Profile**, then the gear icon, then **Scan QR Code** (iPhone: **QR Scan**). You can also tap the QR button at the top of a clan's channel list.
3. Point the camera at the QR code on the computer.
4. The phone shows **Log in on new device**. Tap **Log in**.

The computer opens Mezon with your account.

If the QR code has expired, click it (desktop app) or click the refresh icon (web) to get a new one.

:::warning
Only scan a QR code that you see on your own computer screen. Never confirm a code that someone sent you: they would get into your account.
:::

## Use Mezon on several devices

You can stay logged in on your phone, your computer and in a browser at the same time. To see where you are logged in, or to log out one device, open [Settings → Devices](./personalization-settings.mdx#devices).

To log out of the device you are using, open **Settings** and select **Logout**.
