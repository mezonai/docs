## Tabs at the bottom

| Tab | What you find there |
|---|---|
| **Clans** | Your clans and their channels. |
| **Messages** | Your direct messages and group chats. |
| **Notifications** | Your notifications, for example when someone mentions you. |
| **Profile** | Your name and status, your Mezon Dong wallet, **Edit Profile**, and the gear icon for [settings](./personalization-settings.mdx). |

## Clans tab

- **Left column:** your clans. Tap a clan to open it. Below your clans are **Discover** (the compass, to find public clans) and **+** (to [create a clan](../clan/create-your-own-clan/create-a-clan.mdx)).
- **Top of the clan:** the clan name and the number of members. Tap the name to open the clan menu, for example to invite people.
- **Under the clan name:** **Search**, a QR button to scan a QR code, and a calendar button for the clan's [events](../clan/create-your-own-clan/manage-clan/creating-and-managing-events.mdx).
- **Channel list:** channels grouped in categories. A **#** is a text channel for messages. A speaker icon is a voice channel for talking.

## Left column: your clans

- **Mezon logo** (top): your direct messages and your friends.
- **Clan icons:** click one to open that clan.
- **Discover** (compass): find public clans on the Mezon website.
- **+** (**Create Clan**): [create a clan](../clan/create-your-own-clan/create-a-clan.mdx).

The desktop app also has back and forward arrows at the top.

## Channel list

- **Clan name** (top): click it to open the clan menu, for example **Invite People** or **Clan Settings**.
- **Events**, **Members** and **Channels** of the clan.
- **Categories** with their channels. A **#** is a text channel. A speaker icon is a voice channel.

When you click the Mezon logo, this column shows **Friends** and your **DIRECT MESSAGES** instead.

## Chat in the middle

- **Top bar:** the channel name and buttons for threads, pinned messages, members, notifications and **Search**.
- **Messages** of the channel.
- **Message box** at the bottom: type your message and press Enter.

In a voice channel, the middle shows the people in the call and the buttons for the microphone, the camera and screen sharing.

## Your name, bottom left

- Click your name to see your Mezon Dong balance, **Transfer Funds**, **Transaction History**, **Set Custom Status** and your status.
- Click the gear icon to open [settings](./personalization-settings.mdx).

:::tip
Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) to jump to any clan, channel or person. See [keyboard shortcuts](./shortcuts.mdx).
:::

## Guided tour in the desktop app

The desktop app has a short guided tour. It can start by itself the first time you open the app. To open it again, open the settings and click **Guided tour**. Pick a part, for example **Getting started** or **Voice and calls**. Each part takes about a minute.
