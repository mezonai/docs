# Profile and settings

Your settings control your account, your profile and how Mezon looks and works on each device.

## Open your settings

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap **Profile** at the bottom right.
2. Tap the gear icon at the top right.

<Image src={phoneProfile} alt="The Profile tab with the gear icon marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

Click the gear icon next to your name, at the bottom left.

<Image src={computerGear} alt="Your name at the bottom left with the settings gear marked" />

To close the settings, click **ESC** at the top right.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## What is in the settings

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Account** | See your account details. Set a password. |
| **Chat** | Turn on **Don't receive messages from strangers**. |
| **Friend Requests** | Accept or decline friend requests. |
| **Scan QR Code** (iPhone: **QR Scan**) | Scan a QR code, for example to [log in on a computer](./sign-up-and-log-in.mdx#log-in-on-a-computer-with-your-phone-qr-code). |
| **Devices** (Android only) | See where you are logged in and log out a device. |
| **App Version** | See which version of Mezon you have. |
| **Appearance** | Choose the app theme. |
| **Language** | Choose English or Tiếng Việt. |
| **Logout** | Log out on this phone. |

<Image src={phoneSettings} alt="The Settings screen on Android with the Language row marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Account** | See your display name, username, email and phone number. Set a password or a phone number. |
| **Devices** | See where you are logged in and log out a device. |
| **Profiles** | Change your name, picture and bio, your direct message icon, and your profile in each clan. |
| **Appearance** | Choose a theme. Turn icon animations on or off. |
| **Activity** | Choose whether your friends see what you are doing (**Show My Activity**). |
| **Chat** | Turn on **Don't receive messages from strangers**. You can also turn on **Quick replies**. |
| **Notifications** | Hide the message text in notifications. |
| **Language** | Choose one of 16 languages. |
| **Voice** | Choose your microphone and speaker, set their volume, turn on noise suppression and test your microphone. |
| **Server** | Choose which Mezon server you connect to. Leave **Auto** if you are not sure. This section is not always shown. |
| **Logout** | Log out on this computer or browser. |

The desktop app also has **Advanced** (graphics acceleration and tools for developers), **Guided tour** (a short tour of the screen) and **Check for updates**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Edit your profile {#edit-your-profile}

Your profile shows your display name, your picture (avatar) and a short bio. You can also use a different name and picture in each clan.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap **Profile** at the bottom right.
2. Tap **Edit Profile**.
3. Change your picture, name or bio, then save.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the settings and click **Profiles**.
2. Choose what to change:
   - **User Profile**: your display name, avatar, **About me**, and the **Direct Message Icon** (a picture that replaces the Mezon logo at the top left).
   - **Clan Profiles**: pick a clan, then set a **Clan nickname** and an avatar for that clan.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::note
An avatar file must be 10 MB or smaller. The direct message icon must be 1 MB or smaller.
:::

To set a status (for example **Do Not Disturb**) on a computer, click your name at the bottom left. On a phone, tap **Add Status** on the **Profile** tab.

### User Pro badge and avatar frames

Accounts with **User Pro** show a **PRO** badge on their profile. In the desktop app and the web app, User Pro also unlocks exclusive **avatar decoration frames** that you can choose when editing your profile. See [Pro plan](./pro-plan.mdx).

## Chat {#chat}

Turn on **Don't receive messages from strangers** to let only your friends start a conversation with you. People who are not your friends then see **You can't send a message to this user.**

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

Open the settings, tap **Chat**, then turn on **Don't receive messages from strangers**.

<Image src={phoneChat} alt="The Chat settings with the Don't receive messages from strangers switch marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

Open the settings, click **Chat**, then turn on **Don't receive messages from strangers**.

<Image src={computerChat} alt="The Chat settings on the web with the Don't receive messages from strangers switch marked" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Devices {#devices}

You can be logged in on several phones, computers and browsers at the same time. **Devices** lists them, with the one you are using now under **Current Device**.

1. Open the settings.
2. Select **Devices**.
3. To log out a device, select **Log out of this device** next to it.

<Image src={computerDevices} alt="The Devices settings on the web with the Devices item marked" />

:::warning
If you see a device you do not know, log it out and change your password.
:::

**Devices** is in the web app, the desktop app and the Android app. The iPhone app does not have it.

## Theme

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

<Image src={phoneTheme} alt="The App theme screen with a chat preview and the theme choices" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

Open the settings, click **Appearance**, then click one of the colored circles under **Theme**. Point at a circle to see its name, for example **Dark Theme** or **Light Theme**.

<Image src={computerTheme} alt="The Appearance settings on the web with the theme circles marked" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Language

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

Open the settings, tap **Language**, then choose **English** or **Tiếng Việt**. The phone apps have these two languages.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

Open the settings, click **Language**, then choose a language. The web app and the desktop app have 16 languages, including English and Tiếng Việt.

<Image src={computerLanguage} alt="The Language settings with Tiếng Việt marked" />

The desktop app opens in English the first time. Change the language once and it stays.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
