# User Pro and Clan Pro

Mezon offers optional annual upgrades for individual accounts (<PlanBadge plan="user-pro" />) and clans (<PlanBadge plan="clan-pro" />). Everyday chatting, messaging, and joining clans remain free.

- **User Pro:** **500,000 Mezon Dong / year** (~500,000 VND) — upgrades your personal account.
- **Clan Pro:** **2,000,000 Mezon Dong / year** (~2,000,000 VND) — upgrades one specific clan.

:::info
Pro plans are paid as a **one-time 1-year payment** in **Mezon Dong** (or activated with a **Pro coupon code**). Plans do not auto-renew. Upgrading is currently available in the **Desktop app** and **Web app**; mobile purchasing is not yet available.
:::

## Clan Free vs Clan Pro {#clan-pro}

Only the **clan owner** can purchase or redeem a coupon for **Clan Pro**.

| Feature | Clan Free | Clan Pro |
|---|---|---|
| **Price and billing cycle** | Free | 2,000,000 Mezon Dong / year (~2,000,000 VND / year) |
| **Where to upgrade** | Not applicable | **Clan Settings → Upgrade Clan Pro** (Desktop/Web clan menu) |
| **Voice channel (SFU call)** | Up to 100 users | Up to 500 users |
| **External call / event** | Under 100 users, up to 1 hour | External events up to 500 users, unlimited duration |
| **Sound effects** | Free plan limit | Longer sound effects (exact limit follows server plan configuration) |
| **Storage** | Free plan limit | Higher storage capacity and history retention (follows server plan configuration) |
| **Livestream** | Up to 1 hour, under 100 viewers | Longer livestream duration, under 500 viewers |
| **Visual perks & status** | **Upgrade Clan Pro** prompt | Active plan status, **PRO** clan badge, and exclusive clan decoration frames |

## User Free vs User Pro {#user-pro}

| Feature | User Free | User Pro |
|---|---|---|
| **Price and billing cycle** | Free | 500,000 Mezon Dong / year (~500,000 VND / year) |
| **Where to upgrade** | Not applicable | **Profile → Upgrade Account** (Desktop/Web footer profile popup) |
| **Sound effects** | Free plan limit | Longer sound effects (exact limit follows server plan configuration) |
| **Storage** | Free plan limit | Higher storage capacity and history retention (follows server plan configuration) |
| **External call** | Under 100 users, up to 1 hour | Create external calls up to 500 users, unlimited duration |
| **Visual perks & status** | **Upgrade** prompt | Active plan status, **PRO** profile badge, and exclusive Pro avatar frames (Desktop) |
| **Creating a new clan** | Normal clan | Normal clan (upgrade each clan separately with Clan Pro) |

:::note
A **User Pro** account does not automatically make your clans **Clan Pro**. Any new clan you create starts as a normal clan.
:::

## How to upgrade

Make sure your account has enough [Mezon Dong](../mezon-dong/what-is-mezon-dong.mdx) before upgrading, or have a valid coupon code ready.

<Tabs groupId="pro-target" queryString>
<TabItem value="clan" label="Upgrade Clan Pro">

Only the **clan owner** sees this option.

**Desktop app**

1. Open the clan you own and click the **clan name** at the top of the channel list.
2. Click **Upgrade Pro Plan**. The **CLAN SETTINGS › UPGRADE CLAN PRO** screen opens showing the **Free**, **User Pro**, and **Clan Pro** cards.
3. Click **Upgrade Clan Pro**.
4. Review the amount and confirm to activate **Clan Pro** for 1 year.

**Web app**

1. Open the clan you own and click the **clan name** at the top of the channel list.
2. Click **Enable Pro Plan**.

<Image src={proClanMenu} alt="The clan dropdown menu with Enable Pro Plan marked" width={360} />

3. Confirm the annual amount and click **Confirm purchase**.

<Image src={proClanModal} alt="The Confirm Clan Pro Purchase dialog showing 2,000,000 tokens per year with Confirm purchase marked" width={540} />

After payment completes, the **PRO** badge appears next to your clan name and the plan shows as active.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="user" label="Upgrade User Pro">

**Desktop app**

1. Click your **name / avatar** at the bottom left to open the profile popup.
2. Click **Upgrade Pro Plan**. The **PROFILE › UPGRADE ACCOUNT** screen opens.
3. Click **Upgrade User Pro**.
4. Review the amount and confirm to activate **User Pro** for 1 year.

**Web app**

1. Click your **name / avatar** at the bottom left to open the profile popup.
2. Click **Enable User Pro Plan**.
3. Confirm the annual amount and click **Confirm purchase**.

<Image src={proUserModal} alt="The profile popup showing Enable User Pro Plan and the Confirm User Pro Plan Purchase dialog for 500,000 tokens per year" />

After payment completes, the **PRO** badge appears on your profile. On Desktop, you can also pick an exclusive Pro avatar frame in [Profile and settings](./profile-and-settings.mdx).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Redeem a Pro coupon code {#coupon}

If you received a Mezon Pro coupon code:

1. Open the **Upgrade Pro Plan** window in the **Desktop app** (from your profile popup for a User coupon, or from the clan menu for a Clan coupon).
2. Enter your coupon code in the coupon box.
3. Click **Redeem**.

Each coupon code applies to its designated scope (**User** or **Clan**) and can be redeemed once per user or clan before its expiry date.

## Expiration and renewal

- Each purchase or coupon activates Pro for **1 year** from activation.
- Plans **do not auto-renew**. When the 1-year period ends, the account or clan returns to the Free plan until you upgrade again.
