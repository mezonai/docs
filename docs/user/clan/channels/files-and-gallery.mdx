# Files and gallery

You can send documents, pictures, videos and other files in a text channel, a thread or a direct message, and find them again later.

## Send a file

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the channel or thread.
2. Tap **+** to the left of the message box.
3. Tap **Upload a file** and choose the file.
4. Tap send.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the channel or thread.
2. Click **+** to the left of the message box.
3. Click **Upload a File**.

<Image src={filesComputer01} alt="The plus menu next to the message box with Upload a File" />

4. Choose the file and click **Open**.
5. Add a message if you want, then press **Enter**.

You can also drag files into the chat, or paste them in the message box.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::note
On a computer, one message can have up to 50 files. A picture can be up to 50 MB, and any other file up to 1024 MB.
:::

## Find a file that was sent

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap the channel name at the top.
2. Open the **Files** tab. Documents and audio files are listed separately.

<Image src={filesPhone01} alt="The Files tab of a channel with one PDF file" width={270} />

3. Tap a file to open it.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the **Files** button at the top right of the channel.
2. Type part of the name in **Search for File Name** to filter the list. Each file shows who shared it and when.
3. Click **Download** to save a file to your computer.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Gallery {#gallery}

The Gallery shows the pictures and videos sent in a text channel or thread.

### Send a picture or a video

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the channel or thread.
2. Tap **+** to the left of the message box.
3. Tap the pictures or videos you want to send.
4. Tap send.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the channel or thread.
2. Click **+** to the left of the message box, then **Upload a File**.
3. Choose the picture or video and click **Open**.
4. Add a message if you want, then press **Enter**.

You can also drag a picture into the chat, or paste it in the message box.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

### Browse the Gallery

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap the channel name at the top.
2. Open the **Media** tab. Pictures and videos are listed separately.

<Image src={galleryPhone01} alt="The Media tab of a channel with one picture" width={270} />

3. Tap a picture or a video to open it.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the **Gallery** button at the top right of the channel.
2. To show only pictures or only videos, choose **Images** or **Videos** instead of **All**.
3. To show a period, click **Sent Date**.
4. Pick a **From Date** and a **To Date**.
5. Click **Apply**. **Clear all** removes the dates.
6. Click a picture to open it. In the viewer you can zoom, rotate and download it.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
