# Channels

A clan is organized into **categories**, and each category holds **channels** where members write, talk and watch together. A new clan has a category called **PUBLIC CHANNELS** with a text channel called **general**. A clan started from a template also gets the template's categories and channels.

<Image src={clanStructureComputer01} alt="The channel list of a clan: the PUBLIC CHANNELS category with text, voice and stream channels, two of them private with a lock on the icon" />

## Channel types

| Type | Icon in the channel list | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [**Text**](./text-channel.mdx) | # | Messages, pictures, files, threads and topics |
| [**Voice**](./voice-channel.mdx) | Speaker | Talking, video, screen sharing and interactive apps |
| [**Stream**](./stream-channel.mdx) | Broadcast waves | Going live and watching one person's live stream |

A private text channel shows a **#** with a small lock. A private voice channel shows a speaker with a small lock. Only the clan owner, administrators and the members and roles added to it can see a private channel. Stream channels are always open to every member of the clan.

## Who can create categories and channels

- **Categories:** the clan owner and roles with the **Administrator** or **Manage Clan** permission.
- **Channels:** the clan owner and roles with the **Administrator**, **Manage Clan** or **Manage Channels** permission. Other members do not see the create button.

A clan can have up to 1000 channels, threads included.

## Create a channel {#create-a-channel}

The clan owner and roles with the **Administrator**, **Manage Clan** or **Manage Channels** permission can create channels.

<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

<ShotSteps>
<Shot src={channelsPhone01} alt="The category menu with Create channel marked">Open your clan. Press and hold a category name, for example **PUBLIC CHANNELS**. Tap **Create channel**.</Shot>
<Shot src={channelsPhone02} alt="The Create Channel screen with a name typed and the Voice type marked">Type the channel name. Choose **Text**, **Voice** or **Stream**. To make a text or voice channel private, turn on **Private Channel**. Tap **Create**.</Shot>
</ShotSteps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open your clan.
2. Point at a category name and click the **+** next to it.

<Image src={channelsComputer01} alt="The plus button next to the PUBLIC CHANNELS category" />

3. Choose the type: **Text**, **Voice** or **Stream**.
4. Type the channel name.
5. To make a text or voice channel private, turn on **Private Channel**.
6. Click **Create Channel**.

<Image src={channelsComputer02} alt="The create channel dialog with the Text type selected and a name typed in" />

You can also right-click a channel in the category and choose **Create Text Channel** (**Create Voice Channel** on a voice channel).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The new channel appears in the category.

Rules for channels:

- A channel name can have up to 64 characters. Use letters, numbers, spaces, `_` or `-`. It cannot start with a space, `_` or `-`.
- Two channels in the same category cannot have the same name.
- Stream channels cannot be private. Every member of the clan can see them.
- A clan can have up to 1000 channels, threads included.

## Change a channel's settings

The clan owner and roles that can manage channels can change a channel's settings.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the channel name.
2. Tap **Edit Channel**.

<Image src={channelsPhone03} alt="The channel menu with Edit Channel marked" width={270} />

3. Change the name or the topic, or tap **Change Category** to move the channel.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the channel name.
2. Click **Edit Channel**.

<Image src={channelsComputer03} alt="The channel menu with Edit Channel" />

3. Choose a tab on the left and make your changes.

<Image src={channelsComputer04} alt="The Overview tab of a channel's settings with Channel Name, Channel Topic, Age-Restricted Channel and Hide After Inactivity" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

On a computer, the settings have these tabs. Some tabs show only for some channel types.

| Tab | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | **Channel Name**. **Channel Topic**: tell members what the channel is for (up to 512 characters on a computer). **Age-Restricted Channel** (text channels): members must confirm their age before they see the messages. **Hide After Inactivity**: threads that have no new messages for this time no longer show in the channel list. |
| **Category** | The category the channel is in. |
| **Permissions** | Text and voice channels. Make the channel private, and choose which members and roles can see it and what they can do. See [Roles and permissions](../manage/roles-and-permissions.mdx). |
| **Integrations** | Text channels. Create webhooks that post messages into the channel. See [Channel webhooks](../../../developer/webhooks/channel-webhook.mdx). |
| **Quick Actions** | Text channels. **Flash Messages**: save a text and send it by typing `/` and its name. **Quick Menus**: commands that trigger a bot. |
| **Streaming** | Stream channels. Copy the **WHIP Server URL** and **Bearer Token** for OBS Studio, and set a **Stream Thumbnail** shown while no one is live (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, up to 10 MB). See [Stream channel](./stream-channel.mdx). |
| **Delete Channel** | Delete the channel. The clan's welcome channel cannot be deleted. To hide a text channel without losing its messages, [archive it](./text-channel.mdx#archive-a-channel) instead. |

:::danger
Deleting a channel cannot be undone.
:::
