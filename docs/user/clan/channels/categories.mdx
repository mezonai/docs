# Categories

A category groups related channels in the channel list. The clan owner and roles with the **Administrator** or **Manage Clan** permission can create, rename and delete categories.

## Create a category

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open your clan.
2. Tap the clan name at the top of the channel list.
3. Tap **Create Category**.

<Image src={clanCategoryPhone01} alt="The clan menu with Create Category marked" width={270} />

4. Type the category name.
5. Tap **Create**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open your clan.
2. Click the clan name at the top of the channel list.
3. Click **Create Category**.

<Image src={clanCategoryComputer01} alt="The clan menu with Create Category at the top" />

4. Type the category name.
5. Click **Create Category**.

<Image src={clanCategoryComputer02} alt="The Create Category dialog with a name typed in" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The new category appears in the channel list. A category name can have up to 64 characters. Use letters, numbers, spaces, `_` or `-`. It cannot start with a space, `_` or `-`. Two categories in one clan cannot have the same name.

:::note
A category with no channels is hidden unless **Show Empty Categories** is turned on in the clan menu.
:::

## Manage a category

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

Press and hold the category name. You can:

- **Mark as Read**: clear the unread marks of all channels in the category.
- **Create channel**: add a channel to the category (only if you can manage channels).

**iPhone:** only **Create Channel**.

To rename or delete a category, use a computer.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

Right-click the category name. You can:

- **Mark As Read**: clear the unread marks of all channels in the category.
- **Collapse Category** or **Collapse All Categories**: hide the channel names to save space.
- **Mute Category** and **Notification Settings**: choose which messages in the category notify you.
- **Edit Category**: rename the category.
- **Delete Category**: shown only when the category has no channels. Move or delete its channels first.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Move a channel to another category

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the channel name.
2. Tap **Edit Channel**.
3. Tap **Change Category** and choose the new category.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the channel name.
2. Click **Edit Channel**.
3. Open the **Category** tab and choose the new category.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Category order {#category-order}

**Category Order** changes the order of the categories in your channel list, for example to put the categories you use most at the top.

The order is personal. It changes only your own channel list, even if you are the clan owner. Every member can set their own order.

### Change the order on a computer

1. Open **Clan Settings** and click **Category Order**.
2. Drag a category up or down.
3. Click **Save Changes**. To go back without saving, click **Reset**.

<Image src={computerCategoryOrder} alt="The Category Order page with the categories PROJECTS and PUBLIC CHANNELS" />

The phone apps do not have **Category Order**.
