# Create a clan

Anyone with a Mezon account can create a clan. You become the clan owner.

## Create the clan

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. In the clan list on the left, tap **+**.
2. Tap **Create My Own**. Or tap a template under **Start from a template**.
3. Type the clan name.
4. To add a picture, tap **Upload** and choose an image.
5. Tap **Create Clan**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. In the clan bar on the far left, click **+** (**Create Clan**).
2. Click **Create My Own**. Or click a template under **START FROM A TEMPLATE**.

<Image src={computerChoose} alt="The Create Your Clan dialog with Create My Own and the list of templates" />

3. Type the name under **CLAN NAME**.
4. To add a picture, click **Upload** and choose an image.
5. Click **Create**.

<Image src={computerName} alt="The Customize Your Clan dialog with a clan name filled in and the Create button" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Your new clan opens. It has one category, **PUBLIC CHANNELS**, with the text channel **general**. A template also adds its own categories and channels.

In a web browser, the **general** channel shows a short start list: **Invite your friends**, **Send your first message**, **Download the Mezon App** and **Create your channel**.

## Templates

A template creates channels for a purpose, so you do not start empty. The templates are **Gaming**, **Friends**, **Study Group**, **School Club**, **Local Community** and **Artists & Creators**. **Create My Own** starts with the **general** channel only.

You can rename, add or delete channels later.

## Name and picture rules

- The name can have up to 64 characters.
- Use letters (Vietnamese letters too), numbers, spaces, `_` and `-`. The name cannot start with a space, `_` or `-`.
- In a web browser you can also use emoji in the name. The phone apps and the desktop app do not accept emoji.
- No other clan in Mezon may use the same name.
- The picture is optional. Use an image of 1 MB or less.
- If you skip the picture, Mezon gives the clan a picture automatically.

You can change the name and the picture later in [Clan Settings → Overview](./manage-clan/overview-settings/overview.mdx).

:::note
You can be in up to 50 clans, including the ones you created. When you reach 50, leave a clan before you create a new one.
:::
