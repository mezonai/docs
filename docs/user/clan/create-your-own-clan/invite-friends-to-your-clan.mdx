# Invite people to your clan

Every member of a clan can invite people. Send an invite link or a QR code, or invite your Mezon friends directly.

## Invite people

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

<ShotSteps>
<Shot src={phoneMenu} alt="The clan menu with Invite marked">Open the clan and tap the clan name at the top. Tap **Invite**.</Shot>
<Shot src={phoneSheet} alt="The Invite a friend sheet with Share Invite, Copy Link and QR Code">Choose how to send the invite (see the list below).</Shot>
</ShotSteps>

- **Copy Link**, then paste the link in a message.
- **Share Invite**, to send the link with another app on your phone.
- **QR Code**, to show a code that people scan with their phone. Tap the download icon to save it as a picture, or the share icon to send it with another app.
- **Invite** next to a friend's name, to send the link to that friend in Mezon. The button then shows **Invited**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the clan and click the clan name at the top of the channel list.
2. Click **Invite People**.

<Image src={computerMenu} alt="The clan menu with Invite People" />

3. Choose one:
   - Click **Copy** to copy the invite link, then paste it in a message.
   - Click **Invite** next to a friend's name. Mezon sends the link to that friend in a direct message, and the button shows **Sent**.
   - Click **COPY QR** to open a QR code of the link.

<Image src={computerDialog} alt="The Invite friends dialog: a list of friends with Invite buttons, and the invite link with the Copy button" />

To find a friend quickly, type in **Search for friends**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Use the QR code on a computer

The QR card shows the clan name, the number of members and the code. Under the code you can:

- **Copy image**: copy the QR picture, to paste it in a chat or a document.
- **Download**: save the QR picture to your computer, for example to print it.
- **Copy link**: copy the invite link.

<Image src={computerQr} alt="The QR card of a clan invite with the Copy image, Download and Copy link buttons" width={400} />

The pencil at the top right changes the picture in the middle of the code: a picture from your computer, your own avatar, or the Mezon logo.

**Desktop app:** the QR window has only **Copy QR** and **Cancel**.

## What the invited person sees

- On a phone, the clan name and the number of members, with **Join** and **No, thanks**.
- On a computer, the same details with **Accept Invite**.

Someone without a Mezon account logs in first. Logging in for the first time creates the account. See [Join and explore clans](../join-and-explore-clans.mdx).

:::warning
Anyone who has the link can join your clan. Share it only with the people you want. If a link stops working, open **Invite** again to get a link that works.
:::
