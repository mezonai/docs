# Create and manage events

An event tells the clan what will happen and when, for example a weekly meeting. Every member of a clan can create an event.

## Where an event takes place

When you create an event, you choose one of three places:

- **Voice Channel**: a voice channel of the clan.
- **Somewhere Else** (phones: **Somewhere else**): a text channel, a link or a real address.
- **Create External Event**: a separate online meeting room. People can join it with the link, without logging in to Mezon.

You can also choose who the event is for:

- With no channel chosen, it is a **Clan Event**, open to everyone in the clan.
- If you choose a channel under **Who are audiences?**, it is a **Channel Event** for the members of that channel. On phones this field is called **Announcement channel (optional)**.
- An external event shows the label **External Event**.

## Create an event

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan and tap the clan name at the top.
2. Tap **Create Event**.
3. Choose **Voice Channel**, **Somewhere else** or **Create External Event**. Pick the voice channel, or type the address. Tap **Next**.
4. Type the **Event name**. Choose the **Date**, **Start time** and **End time**. To repeat the event, choose **Repeat**. Tap **Next**.
5. Check the preview and tap **Create Event**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the clan and click **Events** at the top of the channel list.
2. Click **Create Event**.

<Image src={computerList} alt="The Events window with the Create Event button" />

3. Under **Where is your event?**, choose **Voice Channel**, **Somewhere Else** or **Create External Event**. Pick the voice channel, or type the location. To limit the event to one channel, pick it under **Who are audiences?**. Click **Next**.

<Image src={computerWhere} alt="Step 1 of the event: Voice Channel chosen with the Meeting Room channel, and the Next button" />

4. Type the **EVENT TOPIC**. Choose the **START DATE**, **START TIME** and **END TIME**. To repeat the event, choose an option in **EVENT FREQUENCY**. You can add a **DESCRIPTION** and a **COVER IMAGE**. Click **Next**.

<Image src={computerInfo} alt="Step 2 of the event: topic, date, start and end time, frequency, description and cover image" />

5. Check the preview and click **Create Event**.

<Image src={computerReview} alt="Step 3 of the event: the preview of the event and the Create Event button" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The event appears in the list of events. On a computer, the number of upcoming events shows next to **Events**.

### Rules for the event details

- The start time must be in the future, and the end time after the start time.
- The topic cannot contain these characters: `<` `>` `,` `/` `"` `\` `'` and the backtick.
- A repeating event can be weekly, every other week, monthly, yearly, or every weekday (Monday to Friday).
- The location can have up to 100 characters.
- The cover image can be up to 1 MB.
- On a computer, the description can have up to 255 characters.

## See the events of a clan

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

- **Android:** open the clan and tap **Events** in the channel list.
- **iPhone:** open the clan and tap the event icon at the top of the channel list, next to the search box.

Tap an event to see its details and who is interested.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

Click **Events** at the top of the channel list. Each event shows its date and time, its topic, where it takes place and how many people are interested.

- Click **Interested** to show that you plan to join. Click **Uninterested** to undo it.
- Click **Share** to send the event to your friends.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

An event starts by itself at its start time.

:::tip
To remind people, copy the event link and post it in a channel.
:::

## Edit, cancel or copy the link of an event

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the list of events.
2. Open the menu of the event.
3. Choose an action:
   - **Android:** **Edit Event**, **Cancel Event**, **Copy Event Link** or **Copy Content**.
   - **iPhone:** **Edit Event**, **Delete Event**, **Copy Event Link** or **Copy Content**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click **Events** at the top of the channel list.
2. On the event, click the three dots (**...**).
3. Choose **Edit Event**, **Cancel Event** or **Copy Event Link**.

<Image src={computerMenu} alt="The menu of an event with Edit Event, Cancel Event and Copy Event Link" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

**Copy Event Link** is there for everyone. On a computer, **Edit Event** and **Cancel Event** appear only for the person who created the event, the clan owner, and members with the **Administrator** or **Manage Clan** permission.

:::danger
A cancelled event cannot be restored. Create it again if you need it.
:::
