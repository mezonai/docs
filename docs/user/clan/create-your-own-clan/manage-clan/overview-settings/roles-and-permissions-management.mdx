# Roles and permissions

A role is a group of members with the same permissions, color and icon. Every member has the **@everyone** role, shown as **Everyone**. To give some members more rights, create a role for them.

The clan owner and members with the **Manage Clan** permission can manage roles.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Manage the whole clan, just below the clan owner. |
| **Manage Clan** | Change clan settings, manage roles and channels, and oversee members. |
| **Manage Channels** | Create, edit and delete channels. |
| **Manage Threads** | Create, edit and delete threads in a channel, also private threads. |
| **Send Messages** | Send messages in public channels, also in their threads and topics. |
| **Delete Messages** | Delete messages in public channels, also in their threads and topics. |
| **View Channels** | See the public channels. |

**Administrator** and **Manage Clan** differ in a few places. For example, only **Administrator** shows the **Channels** page at the top of the channel list.

### The clan owner

The person who creates a clan is its owner. The owner can do everything, cannot be removed, and is the only one who can [transfer the ownership](../../transferring-clan-ownership.mdx) or [delete the clan](./delete-clan.mdx).

## Set what every member may do

The **Everyone** role applies to all members. In a new clan, **Send Messages** and **View Channels** are on.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan, tap the clan name, then tap **Settings**.
2. Under **User Management**, tap **Roles**.
3. Tap the default permissions at the top of the list.
4. Turn the permissions on or off, then save.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open **Clan Settings** and click **Roles**.
2. Click **Default permissions**.
3. Click the **Permissions** tab.
4. Turn the permissions on or off.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

<Image src={computerPermissions} alt="The Permissions tab of the Everyone role, with Send Messages and View Channels turned on" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Create a role

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open **Settings**, then **Roles**.
2. Tap **+**.
3. Step 1: type the **Role Name**, pick a color and tap **Create**.
4. Step 2: turn on the permissions of the role and tap **Next**.
5. Step 3: choose the members who get the role and tap **Finish**.

You can tap **Skip this step** in steps 2 and 3, and do it later.

<Image src={phoneCreateRole} alt="Step 1 of 3, Create a new role, with a role name typed and Create marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open **Clan Settings** and click **Roles**.
2. Click **Create Role**.

<Image src={computerRoles} alt="The Roles page with Default permissions, the list of roles and the Create Role button" />

3. On the **Display** tab, type the **ROLE NAME** and pick a **ROLE COLOR**. To add a **ROLE ICON**, click **Choose image**.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

<Image src={computerDisplay} alt="The Display tab of a new role with its name, color and icon, and the Save Changes button" />

5. On the **Permissions** tab, turn on what the role may do, then click **Save Changes**.
6. On the **Manage Members** tab, click **Add members**, tick the members and click **Add**.

<Image src={computerAddMembers} alt="The Add members window with a member ticked and the Add button" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The role icon can be an image under 256 KB, or an emoji of the clan. Use an image of at least 64 x 64 pixels.

## Change the order of roles

On a computer, drag a role up or down in the list on the **Roles** page. A member who has several roles shows the color of the highest one.

## Edit or delete a role

- **Computer:** click the role on the **Roles** page, change it and click **Save Changes**. To delete it, click the red bin next to the role.
- **Phone:** tap the role, change it and tap **Save**. To delete it, tap **Delete Role**.

You can edit only roles that are lower than your own highest role.

## Permissions for one channel

A channel can have its own rules, for example a channel that only some members can see, or a channel where only some members can write.

- **Computer:** right-click the channel, click **Edit Channel**, then open **Permissions**. Use **Private Channel** to choose who can see it, or **Advanced View** to set a permission for one role or one member. **Prevent Everyone to Send messages** turns the channel into a read-only channel, with exceptions you choose.
- **Phone:** press and hold the channel, tap **Edit Channel**, then open the channel permissions. They have a basic and an advanced tab.

See [Channels](../clan-structure/channels/index.mdx) for more about private channels.
