# Enable community

Community makes your clan public. People can find it on [Discover](../../../join-and-explore-clans.mdx) and join it, and it gets its own web address.

The clan owner and members with the **Administrator** permission can turn Community on.

## Turn on Community

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan, tap the clan name, then tap **Settings**.
2. Tap **Enable Community**.
3. Tap **Enable Community** again to start.
4. Add a banner, a description, the text about your community and a vanity URL. On an iPhone, also choose the hashtags.
5. Tap **Enable & Save**.

<Image src={phoneForm} alt="The Community Settings form with Enable & Save marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open **Clan Settings** and click **Enable Community** (desktop app: **Clan Community**).
2. Click **Enable Community**.

<Image src={computerStart} alt="The Enable Community page with the Enable Community button" />

3. Fill in every field. All of them are required:
   - **Community Banner**: click **Upload Banner** and choose an image of up to 10 MB.
   - **Community Description**: a short text of up to 300 characters.
   - **About Community**: a longer text of up to 3,000 characters.
   - **Community Hashtags**: pick 1 to 6 topics. **Community** is already picked.
   - **Vanity URL**: the end of your community's web address, up to 32 characters. Use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.

<Image src={computerForm} alt="The top of the Enable Community form: banner, description and About Community" />

4. Click **Enable & Save**.

<Image src={computerFormEnd} alt="The end of the form: hashtags, the vanity URL and the Enable & Save button" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Your clan is now a community. In **Clan Settings**, the item **Enable Community** becomes **Community Overview**.

The address of your community page is `mezon.ai/clans/clan/` followed by your vanity URL. For example, the vanity URL `green-garden` gives `mezon.ai/clans/clan/green-garden`.

The hashtags to choose from are **Gaming**, **Technology**, **Education**, **Entertainment**, **Music**, **Art**, **Crypto**, **Anime & Manga**, **Sports** and **Community**.

## Change the community details or turn Community off

1. Open **Clan Settings** and click **Community Overview**.
2. Change the details and click **Save Changes**.

To make the clan private again, click **Disable Community**. In a web browser, it takes effect at once, without asking you to confirm. The clan is then no longer listed on Discover.
