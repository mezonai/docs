# Integrations

**Integrations** lists the webhooks of your clan. A webhook is a web address that another app or website can call to post a message in Mezon, for example a build server or a form.

The clan owner and members with the **Manage Clan** or **Manage Channels** permission can see **Integrations**.

## Two kinds of webhooks

- **Webhooks** post messages in one channel of the clan.
- **Clan Webhooks** send a message to one member of the clan.

## Create a webhook on a computer

1. Open **Clan Settings** and click **Integrations**.
2. Click **Create Webhook** or **Create Clan Webhook**. If you already have webhooks, click **View Webhook** or **View Clan Webhook**, then **New Webhook** or **New Clan Webhook**.

<Image src={computerIntegrations} alt="The Integrations page with Webhooks and Clan Webhooks and their Create buttons" />

3. Open the new webhook. Change its name (up to 64 characters), its picture and, for a channel webhook, its **CHANNEL**.
4. If you changed something, click **Save Changes** in the bar at the bottom.
5. Click **Copy Webhook URL**, and paste the URL in the other app.

:::warning
Anyone who has the webhook URL can post in your clan. Keep it secret. If it leaks, click **Reset Token** and use the new URL.
:::

To remove a webhook, open it and click **Delete Webhook**.

**Phones:** **Integrations** is in **Settings** too.
