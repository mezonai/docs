# Emoji, stickers and sound effects

A clan can have its own emoji, image stickers and sound effects. Everyone in the clan can use them. Any member can add new ones in **Clan Settings**.

## File rules

| | Emoji | Image sticker | Sound effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| File types | JPEG, PNG, GIF | PNG, JPG, APNG, GIF | MP3, WAV |
| Largest file | 256 KB | 512 KB | 1 MB |
| Picture size | 128 x 128 pixels recommended | exactly 320 x 320 pixels; Mezon resizes PNG and JPG for you | — |
| Name | 3 to 64 characters: letters, numbers, `_` and `-` | same as emoji | same as emoji |

A clan can have up to 250 emoji and 250 image stickers. On the phone apps, you choose a part of 1 to 10 seconds of a sound.

## Add an emoji, a sticker or a sound

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan, tap the clan name, then tap **Settings**.
2. Tap **Emoji**, **Sticker** or **Sound Effect**.
3. Tap the upload button, for example **Upload Emoji** or **Upload Sound**.
4. Choose the file. For a sound, choose the part to keep.
5. Type a name.
6. Tap **Upload**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open **Clan Settings**.
2. Under **EMOTIONS**, click **Emoji**, **Image Stickers** or **Sound Effect**.
3. Click the upload button: **Upload Emoji**, **Upload Sticker** or **Upload Voice Sticker**.

<Image src={computerEmoji} alt="The Emoji page with the upload rules and the Upload Emoji button" />

<Image src={computerSticker} alt="The Image Stickers page with the Upload Sticker button" />

<Image src={computerSound} alt="The Sound Effect page with the Upload Voice Sticker button" />

4. Click **Browse** and choose the file.
5. Type a name. For a sound, you can also set the **Sound Volume**.
6. Click the upload button in the window.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The new item appears in the list.

## Sell an emoji or a sticker

When you upload an emoji or an image sticker, you can tick **This is for sale**. Members then pay with Mezon Dong to unlock it before they can use it. Items for sale do not count toward the limit of 250.

## Rename or delete

- **Computer:** click **Delete** on the item in the list, then confirm. Deleting cannot be undone.
- **Phone:** tap a sound name to rename it. Swipe a sound to the left to delete it.
