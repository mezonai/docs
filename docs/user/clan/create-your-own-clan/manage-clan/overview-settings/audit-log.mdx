# Audit log

The audit log lists the changes made in your clan: who did what, and when. For example, it shows when someone creates a channel, creates an event or changes a role.

The clan owner and members with the **Manage Clan** permission can see it.

## Open the audit log

- **Computer:** open **Clan Settings** and click **Audit Log**, under **MODERATION**.
- **Phone:** open the clan, tap the clan name, tap **Settings**, then tap **Audit Log**.

<Image src={computerAuditLog} alt="The Audit Log with filters by user, by action and by date, and a list of changes" />

Each line shows the member, the action, the item that changed and the time. The newest changes are at the top.

## Filter the list on a computer

- **Filter by User**: show the actions of one member.
- **Filter by Action**: show one kind of action, for example creating a role.
- The date box: show the actions of one day.
