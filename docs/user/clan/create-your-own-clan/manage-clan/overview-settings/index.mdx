# Clan settings

**Clan Settings** holds the options of a clan: its name and picture, roles, emoji, and more. The items you see depend on your role.

## Open Clan Settings

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan and tap the clan name at the top.
2. Tap **Settings**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the clan and click the clan name at the top of the channel list.
2. Click **Clan Settings**.

<Image src={computerMenu} alt="The clan menu with Clan Settings" />

To close the settings, click **ESC** at the top right, or press the Esc key.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## What is in Clan Settings on a computer

<Image src={computerSidebar} alt="The list of items in Clan Settings, from Overview to Delete Clan" width={210} />

| Item | What it is for | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| [**Overview**](./overview.mdx) | Name, picture, banner, system messages, anonymous mode | the clan owner, **Administrator** |
| [**Roles**](./roles-and-permissions-management.mdx) | Roles and permissions | the clan owner, **Manage Clan** |
| [**Category Order**](./category-order.mdx) | Your own order of the categories | every member |
| **Archived Channels** | Bring back archived channels (see below) | the clan owner, **Administrator**, **Manage Clan** |
| [**Emoji**, **Image Stickers**, **Sound Effect**](./emoji-image-and-voice-stickers.mdx) | The clan's own emoji, stickers and sounds | every member |
| [**Integrations**](./integrations.mdx) | Webhooks | the clan owner, **Manage Clan** or **Manage Channels** |
| [**Audit Log**](./audit-log.mdx) | History of admin actions | the clan owner, **Manage Clan** |
| [**Onboarding**](./new-member-guide.mdx) | Guide and questions for new members | the clan owner, **Manage Clan** |
| [**Enable Community**](./enable-community.mdx) (later **Community Overview**) | List the clan on Discover | the clan owner, **Administrator** |
| [**Delete Clan**](./delete-clan.mdx) | Delete the clan | the clan owner only |

**Desktop app:** the community item is called **Clan Community**.

## What is in Clan Settings on a phone

The phone apps have fewer items:

- Under **Settings**: **Overview**, **Audit Log**, **Integrations**, **Emoji**, **Sticker**, **Sound Effect** and **Enable Community**.
- Under **User Management**: **Members**, **Roles** and **Invites**.

**Category Order**, **Archived Channels** and **Onboarding** are only on a computer. To delete a clan on a phone, see [Delete a clan](./delete-clan.mdx).

## Restore an archived channel

An archived channel is hidden from the channel list for all members. Its messages are kept. Bring it back on a computer:

1. Open **Clan Settings**.
2. Click **Archived Channels**.
3. Click **Restore** next to the channel.

The channel shows in the channel list again.
