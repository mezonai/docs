A clan is organized into **categories**, and each category holds **channels**. A new clan has a category called **PUBLIC CHANNELS** with a text channel called **general**. A clan started from a template also gets the template's categories and channels.

<Image src={clanStructureComputer01} alt="The channel list of a clan: the PUBLIC CHANNELS category with text, voice and stream channels, two of them private with a lock on the icon" />

## Channel types

| Type | Icon in the channel list | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | # | Messages, pictures, files, threads and topics |
| **Voice** | Speaker | Talking, video and screen sharing |
| **Stream** | Broadcast waves | Watching one person's live video |

A private text channel shows a # with a small lock. A private voice channel shows a speaker with a small lock. Only the clan owner, administrators and the members and roles added to it can see a private channel. Stream channels are always open to every member of the clan.

## Who can create categories and channels

- **Categories:** the clan owner and roles with the **Administrator** or **Manage Clan** permission.
- **Channels:** the clan owner and roles with the **Administrator**, **Manage Clan** or **Manage Channels** permission. Other members do not see the create button.

A clan can have up to 1000 channels. Threads count toward this limit.
