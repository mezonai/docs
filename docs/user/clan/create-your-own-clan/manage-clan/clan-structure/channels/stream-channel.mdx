# Stream channel

In a stream channel, one person is live and the other members of the clan watch. A stream channel has a broadcast icon (a dot with waves) in the channel list. Stream channels are always public: every member of the clan can open them and watch.

## Watch a stream

You can watch on an Android phone, an iPhone, the desktop app and the web app.

<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the stream channel.
2. When someone is live, you see their name. Click **Join stream**.

<Image src={streamComputer01} alt="A stream channel with Mai is live and the Join stream button" />

3. The video starts. Point at the video to show the controls.
4. Use the controls to change the volume or the **Quality**, or to watch in full screen.
5. To stop watching, click the red button in the middle.

<Image src={streamComputer02} alt="A live stream playing, with the volume, quality and full-screen controls and the viewers below" />

To chat while you watch, click **Show Chat** at the top right.

</TabItem>

When no one is live, the channel shows **No one is currently in stream** and you cannot join yet.

The quality can be **Auto**, **High**, **Medium** or **Low**. **Auto** changes the quality to match your connection. Choose **Low** if the video stops often.

The number next to the eye icon is how many people are watching. The pictures under the video show who they are.

## Who can create a stream channel

The clan owner and roles with the **Administrator**, **Manage Clan** or **Manage Channels** permission can create one. They create it like any other channel and choose the type **Stream**. See [Create a channel](./index.mdx#create-a-channel).

Only one person can be live in a stream channel at a time.

On a computer, people who can manage the channel can set a **Stream Thumbnail** in the channel settings, on the **Streaming** tab. Members see this picture while no one is live.
