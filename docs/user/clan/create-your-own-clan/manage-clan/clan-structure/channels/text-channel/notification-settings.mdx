# Notification settings

Each channel can notify you about every message, only about messages that mention you, or not at all. These settings change only your own notifications.

## Choose the notifications of a channel

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the channel name in the channel list.
2. Tap **Notification Settings**.
3. Choose **Use default settings**, **All messages**, **Only @mention** or **Nothing**.

<Image src={notificationSettingsPhone01} alt="The Channel Notification Settings sheet with its four options" width={270} />

To silence the channel for a while, press and hold the channel name, tap **Mute Channel** and choose how long.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the channel.
2. Click the bell (**Notification Settings**) at the top right.

<Image src={notificationSettingsComputer01} alt="The notification menu of a channel with Mute Channel, Use Category Default, All, Only @mention and Nothing" />

3. Choose an option.

You can also right-click the channel name and use **Mute Channel** or **Notification Settings**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

What the options mean:

| Option | You get notified about |
|---|---|
| **Use Category Default** (phone: **Use default settings**) | What the channel's category, or the clan, is set to. |
| **All** (phone: **All messages**) | Every new message. |
| **Only @mention** | Messages that mention you. |
| **Nothing** | No messages. |
| **Mute Channel** | Nothing, for 15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, 8 hours, 24 hours, or until you turn it back on. |

:::tip
In busy channels, choose **Only @mention**. You still see messages meant for you, without a sound for every message.
:::
