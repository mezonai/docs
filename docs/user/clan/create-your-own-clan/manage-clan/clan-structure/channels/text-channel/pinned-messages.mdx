# Pinned messages

Pin a message to keep it easy to find, for example rules, a schedule or an address. Everyone in the channel can see the pinned messages.

## Pin a message

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the message.
2. Tap **Pin Message**.
3. Tap **Yes** to confirm.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the message.
2. Click **Pin Message**.
3. Click **Oh yeah. Pin it** to confirm.

<Image src={pinnedComputer01} alt="The Pin It. Pin It Good. dialog with the Oh yeah. Pin it button" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

A note appears in the chat: "… pinned a message to this channel. See all pinned messages." On a computer, the newest pin also shows in the pin bar under the channel name.

You cannot pin a message inside a topic.

## See and unpin pinned messages

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap the channel name at the top.
2. Open the **Pins** tab.
3. Tap a pinned message to go to it in the chat.
4. To unpin it, tap the **X** next to it.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the **Pinned Messages** button at the top right of the channel.

<Image src={pinnedComputer02} alt="The Pinned Messages list with one pinned message, and the pin note in the chat" />

2. Click **Jump** on a message to go to it in the chat.
3. To unpin a message, click the cross next to it, then click **Unpin it**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
