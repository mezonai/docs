# Inbox

The Inbox collects what needs your attention: messages that mention you, messages you saved, and topics you take part in. On a phone, the Inbox is the **Notifications** tab.

## Open the Inbox

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap **Notifications** at the bottom of the app.
2. Choose a tab: **Mentions**, **Messages**, **Topics** or **For You**.
3. Tap an item to go to the message.

To remove an item, press and hold it and tap **Remove notification**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the **Inbox** button at the top right of a channel.
2. Choose a tab: **For you**, **Messages**, **Mentions** or **Topics**.

<Image src={inboxComputer01} alt="The Inbox with the Mentions tab open and one mention" />

3. Click an item to go to the message. Click the cross on an item to remove it.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

What each tab shows:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Mentions** | Messages that mention you. They stay here for 7 days. |
| **Messages** | Messages you saved with **Add To Inbox**. |
| **Topics** | Topics you take part in. On a computer, a number shows how many new replies you have not read. |
| **For you** | Other notifications, for example about events and streams. |

## Save a message to read later

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the message.
2. Tap **Add To Inbox**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the message.
2. Click **Add To Inbox**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The message appears in the **Messages** tab of the Inbox.
