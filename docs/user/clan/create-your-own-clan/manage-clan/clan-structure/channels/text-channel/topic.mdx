# Topic

A topic is a short discussion about one message. The replies stay in the topic, so the channel stays easy to follow. A topic has no name and cannot be private. For a longer discussion with its own name, use a [thread](./threads.mdx).

Anyone who can send messages in a text channel or a thread can start a topic. Topics are not available in direct messages, group chats, voice or stream channels, or inside another topic.

## Start a topic

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the message.
2. Tap **Topic Discussion**.
3. Type your reply and send it.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the message.
2. Click **Topic Discussion**.

<Image src={topicComputer01} alt="The message menu with Topic Discussion" />

3. The topic opens on the right. Type your reply and press **Enter**.

<Image src={topicComputer02} alt="A topic open on the right, with the first message and one reply" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Under the message, a button now shows how many replies the topic has.

## Open a topic

- **In the chat:** click the replies button under the message. On a phone, tap **View topic** (iPhone: **View Topic**).
- **In the Inbox:** open the **Topics** tab. On a phone, it is in the **Notifications** tab at the bottom of the app.
- **In the pin bar:** on a computer, the newest topic shows under the channel name.

On a computer, a mark shows when a topic has replies you have not read.
