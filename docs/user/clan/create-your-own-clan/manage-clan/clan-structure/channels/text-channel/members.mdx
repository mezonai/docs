# Members

The member list shows the people who can see the channel, split into online and offline.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap the channel name at the top.
2. Open the **Members** tab.
3. Tap a name to open that person's profile.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the **Members** button at the top right of the channel.

<Image src={membersComputer01} alt="A text channel with the member list open on the right, Mai online and three members offline" />

2. Click a name to open that person's profile. From there you can send them a message.
3. Click **Members** again to hide the list.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

On a computer, people with the **Manage Clan** permission can also give a role to a member from that member's profile. They can only give roles that are lower than their own, unless they are the clan owner.
