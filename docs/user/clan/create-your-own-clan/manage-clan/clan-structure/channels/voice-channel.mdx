# Voice channel

In a voice channel, members of the clan talk, turn on their cameras and share their screens. Voice channels have a speaker icon in the channel list. A private voice channel has a speaker with a small lock. Only the clan owner, administrators and the members and roles added to it can see it and join it.

## Join a voice channel

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap the voice channel.
2. Tap **Join as Speaker**.

<Image src={voicePhone01} alt="The voice channel sheet with the Join as Speaker button and its menu open" width={270} />

To only listen, tap the arrow next to the button and choose **Join as audience**.

3. The first time, allow Mezon to use the microphone.
4. Tap the microphone button to turn your microphone on or off.

<Image src={voicePhone02} alt="A voice call on a phone with the controls at the bottom" width={270} />

**iPhone:** you join with your microphone off. Tap the microphone button when you want to talk.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the voice channel.
2. Click **Join as Speaker**.

<Image src={voiceComputer01} alt="The voice channel before joining, with the Join as Speaker button" />

To only listen, click the arrow next to the button and choose **Join as Audience**.

<Image src={voiceComputer02} alt="The join menu with Join as Speaker and Join as Audience" />

3. The first time, allow Mezon to use the microphone. In a web browser, choose **Allow** when the browser asks.
4. Use the buttons at the bottom to turn your microphone or camera on or off, or to share your screen.

<Image src={voiceComputer03} alt="A voice channel after joining, with the control bar at the bottom" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

To leave, tap the red button on a phone. On a computer, click **Disconnect** (desktop app: **Leave**).

:::note
When 20 or more people are already in the channel, the computer apps choose **Join as Audience** for you. You can still pick **Join as Speaker** from the arrow.
:::

## Speaker or audience

- A **speaker** can turn on the microphone and the camera and share the screen.
- The **audience** listens and watches. To say something, hold the push-to-talk control: on a computer, hold the **Space** key; on a phone, press and hold **Hold to talk**. Your microphone turns off when you let go.
- Only speakers can share their screen.

## Controls on a computer

The control bar is at the bottom of the call.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Microphone, camera | Turn them on or off. The small arrow next to each one picks the device. |
| **Share Your Screen** | Show a window or your whole screen to everyone in the call. |
| **Raise Hand** | Show the others that you want to speak. |
| **Add AI agent** | Add the KOMU AI agent to the call. Only for people with the **Manage Channels** permission. |
| **Record call** | Record the call on your own computer. Everyone in the call sees that you are recording. |
| Reactions and sound effects | Send an emoji or a short sound to the call. |
| **Breakout rooms** | Split the call into smaller rooms. Only for people with the **Manage Clan** permission. Each room uses an empty public voice channel of the clan. |
| Full screen | Show the call on the whole screen. |
| **Disconnect** (desktop app: **Leave**) | Leave the call. |

More things you can do:

- Right-click a person's tile and use **User Volume** to make that person louder or quieter, from 0 to 150%. This changes the volume only for you.
- People who can manage the channel can also right-click a tile to **Mute Microphone** or **Kick** that person from the call.
- While you are in a call, the **Voice Connected** panel stays at the bottom left, even when you open another channel. It has the microphone, camera, screen and leave buttons, and a button that copies a link to the channel.

<Image src={voiceComputer04} alt="The Voice Connected panel at the bottom left with the call buttons" />

## Controls on a phone

- At the bottom: camera, microphone, chat, screen sharing and the red leave button.
- At the top: the arrow that makes the call small so you can use the rest of the app, raise hand, the sound output (speaker or earpiece), and the more button with emoji, sounds and noise suppression.
- Speakers can share their phone screen.

## Limits

- Only a limited number of cameras and shared screens can be on at the same time. Mezon tells you when the limit is reached.
- You can be in a call from one device at a time. If you join from another device, the first one is disconnected.
