# Notifications in a clan

You choose how much each clan notifies you. You can set one level for the whole clan, and a different level for a category or a channel. These settings are yours only. They do not change anything for other members.

## Notification levels

| Level | You get a notification for |
|---|---|
| **All** (phones: **All messages**) | every new message |
| **Only @mention** | messages that mention you |
| **Nothing** | no messages |

A channel can also use **Use Category Default** (phones: **Use default settings**). It then follows the level set above it.

:::tip
If you are in many clans, set most of them to **Only @mention**. You still see messages meant for you.
:::

## Set the level for a whole clan

<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

<ShotSteps>

<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. In the clan bar on the far left, right-click the clan icon.
2. Point at **Notification Settings**.
3. Choose **All**, **Only @mention** or **Nothing**.

<Image src={computerClanMenu} alt="The clan icon menu with Notification Settings and the levels All, Only @mention and Nothing" />

You can also click the clan name at the top of the channel list and choose **Notification Settings**. The window has the same three levels under **CLAN NOTIFICATION SETTINGS**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Set a different level for a channel or a category

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the channel.
2. Tap **Notification Settings**.
3. Choose **Use default settings**, **All messages**, **Only @mention** or **Nothing**.

<Image src={phoneChannelMenu} alt="The channel menu with Mute Channel and Notification Settings marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the channel or the category name.
2. Point at **Notification Settings**.
3. Choose a level. For a channel, **Use Category Default** follows the level of its category.

To see all your exceptions in one place, click the clan name, then **Notification Settings**. Under **NOTIFICATION OVERRIDES**, pick a channel or a category and set its level, or mute it.

<Image src={computerDialog} alt="The Notification Setting window with the clan level and the Notification Overrides list" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Mute a channel for a while

Muting stops the notifications of a channel for a time you choose.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the channel.
2. Tap **Mute Channel**.
3. Choose how long: from 15 minutes to 24 hours, or until you turn it back on.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the channel.
2. Point at **Mute Channel**.
3. Choose **For 15 Minutes**, **For 1 Hour**, **For 3 Hours**, **For 8 Hours**, **For 24 Hours** or **Until I turn it back on**.

<Image src={computerMute} alt="The channel menu with Mute Channel and the list of durations" />

To mute a whole category, right-click the category name and choose **Mute Category**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

To turn the notifications on again, open the same menu and choose **Unmute Channel**.

## Default level for new members

On the phone apps, the clan owner and members with the **Administrator** permission can choose the level for members who never chose one. Open the clan, tap the clan name, tap **Settings**, then **Overview**, and choose a level under **Default notifications** (iPhone: **Default Notification Settings**). For a large clan, **Only @mention** is a good choice.

## Desktop app: the number on the app icon

In the desktop app, the number on the Mezon icon in the taskbar or the Dock counts your mentions in clans, your unread direct messages in conversations you did not mute, and your new friend requests.
