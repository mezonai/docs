# Emoji, stickers and sound effects

A clan can have its own emoji, image stickers and sound effects. Everyone in the clan can use them. Any member can add new ones in **Clan Settings**.

## File rules and limits

| | Emoji | Image sticker | Sound effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| File types | JPEG, PNG, GIF | PNG, JPG, APNG, GIF | MP3, WAV |
| Largest file | 256 KB | 512 KB | 1 MB (including Desktop app) |
| Picture size | 128 x 128 pixels recommended | exactly 320 x 320 pixels; Mezon resizes PNG and JPG for you | — |
| Name | 3 to 64 characters: letters, numbers, `_` and `-` | same as emoji | same as emoji |
| Clan limit | up to 250 items | up to 250 items | up to 250 items |

A clan can have up to **250 custom items per type** (250 emoji, 250 image stickers and 250 sound effects). On the phone apps, you choose a clip of 1 to 10 seconds of a sound. For Pro sound effect benefits, see [Clan Pro and User Pro](../../account-and-personalization/pro-plan.mdx).

## Add an emoji, a sticker or a sound

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan, tap the clan name, then tap **Settings**.
2. Tap **Emoji**, **Sticker** or **Sound Effect**.
3. Tap the upload button, for example **Upload Emoji** or **Upload Sound**.
4. Choose the file. For a sound, choose the part to keep.
5. Type a name.
6. Tap **Upload**.

## Sell and unlock emoji or stickers

- **Mark an item for sale:** when you upload an emoji or an image sticker, tick **This is for sale**. Items for sale do not count toward the clan's limit of 250 items per type.
- **Unlock a for-sale item (Android and iPhone):** on Android and iPhone, members can unlock a for-sale emoji or sticker for **500 Mezon Dong**, paid from their Mezon Dong wallet. Once unlocked, you can use it in messages and reactions.

## Rename or delete

- **Computer:** click **Delete** on the item in the list, then confirm. Deleting cannot be undone.
- **Phone:** tap a sound name to rename it. Swipe a sound to the left to delete it.
