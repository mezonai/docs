# Onboarding

Onboarding helps new members find their way in your clan. It has two parts:

- **Questions**: questions with a list of answers, to learn what new members are interested in.
- **Clan Guide**: a welcome message, 3 to 5 first tasks, and resource pages.

The clan owner and members with the **Manage Clan** permission set up onboarding. You do it on a computer: the phone apps do not have these settings.

## Turn on onboarding

1. Open **Clan Settings** and click **Onboarding**. It shows **OFF** while onboarding is off.
2. Click **Enable Onboarding**.

<Image src={computerStart} alt="The Onboarding page with the Enable Onboarding button" />

3. The **Onboarding Settings** window opens. Set up at least one part: click **Set up** next to **Questions**, or **Edit** next to **Clan Guide**.
4. Click **Preview** to see what new members will see.
5. Click **Confirm**.

<Image src={computerSettings} alt="The Onboarding Settings window with Questions, Clan Guide, Preview, Cancel and Confirm" />

If nothing is set up yet, Mezon says **Need at least one item to enable onboarding**.

## Add questions

1. In **Onboarding Settings**, click **Set up** next to **Questions**.
2. Under **Pre-join Questions**, click **Add a Question**.
3. Type the question.
4. Click **Add an Answer**. Type a title, and a description if you like.
5. Click **Save**.

<Image src={computerQuestions} alt="The Questions page with Pre-join Questions and the Add a Question button" />

Each question can have up to 50 answers.

## Set up the clan guide

1. In **Onboarding Settings**, click **Edit** next to **Clan Guide**.
2. Under **Welcome Sign**, write what is special about your clan.
3. Under **New Member To Do's**, click **Add a task**. Type what the member should do, choose the channel, and choose when the task is complete: when the member sends a message there, visits the channel, or reads the task.
4. Add 3 to 5 tasks this way.
5. Under **Resource Pages**, click **Add a resource** to show a read-only channel as a page, for example your rules.

<Image src={computerGuide} alt="The Clan Guide editor with Welcome Sign, New Member To Do's and Add a task" />

:::tip
Make each task concrete. "Chat with the community" is too general. "Introduce yourself" in **#general** tells people what to do. A task needs at least 7 characters, and its channel must be public.
:::

## What members see

Once onboarding is on, members see **Clan Guide** at the top of the channel list. It shows the welcome message, the questions, the tasks and the resources. A member ticks off a task by doing it.

To turn onboarding off, open **Clan Settings** → **Onboarding** and click **Disable Onboarding**.
