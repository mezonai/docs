# Transfer clan ownership

The clan owner can make another member the owner, for example when the person who runs the clan changes. Only the current owner can do this.

## Transfer the ownership

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan and tap the clan name at the top.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **User Management**, tap **Transfer Ownership** (available on both Android and iPhone) and select the member who will be the new owner. You can also open **Members**, tap the member, and tap **Transfer Ownership** under **Actions**.
4. Turn on the switch that says you understand (iPhone: tick the box).
5. Tap **Transfer**.

<ShotSteps plain>
<Shot src={phoneSettingsShortcut} alt="Clan Settings on a phone with Transfer Ownership marked under User Management">From **Clan Settings**: tap **Transfer Ownership** under **User Management**.</Shot>
<Shot src={phoneEditMember} alt="The Edit Member screen with Transfer Ownership marked">Or from a member's screen: **Transfer Ownership** under **Actions**.</Shot>
<Shot src={phoneConfirm} alt="The Transfer Ownership screen with the acknowledgement switch and the Transfer button">Confirm with the switch, then tap **Transfer**.</Shot>
</ShotSteps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the clan and click **Members** at the top of the channel list.
2. Right-click the member who will be the new owner.
3. Click **Transfer Ownership**.

<Image src={computerMenu} alt="The Members page with the menu of a member and Transfer Ownership" />

4. Tick the box **I acknowledge that by transferring ownership of this clan to ..., it officially belongs to them.**
5. Click **Transfer Ownership**.

<Image src={computerDialog} alt="The Transfer Clan Ownership dialog with the box ticked and the Transfer Ownership button" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The other member becomes the clan owner at once.

:::warning
You lose the owner rights right away. You cannot take them back yourself: only the new owner can transfer the clan back to you.
:::
