# What is a clan?

A clan is a shared space in Mezon for a team, a class or a group of friends. People in a clan talk in its channels.

<Image src={clanOverview} alt="A clan on a computer: the clan name at the top, then Events, Members, Channels and the list of channels" />

## What a clan has

- **Channels**, grouped in categories. Text channels are for messages. Voice channels are for talking, video and screen sharing. Stream channels are for going live and watching live streams.
- **Members**: the people who joined the clan.
- **Roles**: they decide what each member may do.
- **Events**, for example a weekly meeting.
- **Clan emoji, stickers and sound effects** that members can use in the clan.
- **Bots and apps** that the clan adds.

## Who can do what

- Anyone with a Mezon account can create a clan. The person who creates it is the **clan owner**.
- You can be in up to 50 clans at the same time.
- Every member can chat, invite people and create events.
- The clan owner, and members whose role allows it, manage channels, roles, members and clan settings.
