# Clan folders

A clan folder groups several clans under one icon in the clan bar. It keeps the clan bar short when you are in many clans.

:::note
Clan folders are available only when you use Mezon in a web browser. The phone apps and the desktop app do not have them. In the desktop app you can drag a clan up or down to change the order of your clans.
:::

## Create a folder

1. In the clan bar on the far left, press and hold a clan icon.
2. Drag it onto another clan icon.
3. Release the mouse button.

The two clans now share one folder icon.

<Image src={folderClosed} alt="The clan bar with a folder that shows the icons of two clans" />

## Open or close a folder

- Click the folder to open it. The clans in it appear below a folder icon.
- Click the folder icon at the top to close it.

## Remove a clan from a folder

1. Open the folder.
2. Point at the clan you want to take out.
3. Click the **X** on its icon.

<Image src={folderOpen} alt="An open folder; the X on a clan icon removes the clan from the folder" />

The clan moves back to the clan bar, outside the folder.

Folders are saved in the web browser you use. In another browser, or on another computer, your clans are not grouped.
