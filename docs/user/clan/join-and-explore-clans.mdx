# Join and explore clans

You join a clan with an invite link from someone in the clan, or from Discover, the list of public clans. You can leave a clan at any time.

:::note
You can be in up to 50 clans. To join another one, leave a clan first.
:::

## Join with an invite link

An invite link looks like `https://mezon.ai/invite/...`.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the invite link, for example in an SMS, an email or a chat.
2. Mezon opens and shows the clan name and the number of members. Tap **Join**.

<Image src={phoneJoin} alt="The invite screen with the clan name, the member count and the Join button marked" width={270} />

If you are not logged in, log in first. Mezon then shows the invite again. To say no, tap **No, thanks**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the invite link in your web browser.
2. If the page asks **Open this link in the Mezon desktop app?**, click **Open app** only if you installed the desktop app. Otherwise, close the question.
3. Click **Accept Invite**.

<Image src={computerJoin} alt="The invite page with the clan name, the member count and the Accept Invite button" />

In a Mezon chat, an invite link shows a card with the clan name and **Join**. In a web browser, click **Join** to join at once.

**Desktop app:** **Join** opens a window. Click **Accept Invite**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The clan appears in your clan list and opens.

If Mezon says **The invitation is not valid or has expired**, ask the person who invited you for a new link.

## Find public clans on Discover

Discover lists the clans whose owners made them public (see [Enable Community](./create-your-own-clan/manage-clan/overview-settings/enable-community.mdx)).

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. In the clan list on the left, tap the **Discover** icon below your clans.
2. Search, or choose a topic.
3. Open a clan and tap **Join Clan**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. In the clan bar on the far left, click **Discover** (the compass icon). The Mezon clans page opens in your web browser.
2. Search, or choose a topic such as **Education**.
3. Open a clan and click **Join Clan**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

To try Mezon in a ready-made clan, see [Sample clans by Mezon](./mezon-experience-clans/index.mdx).

## Leave a clan

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan.
2. Tap the clan name at the top.
3. Tap **Leave Clan**.
4. Confirm.

<Image src={phoneLeave} alt="The clan menu with Leave Clan marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the clan.
2. Click the clan name at the top of the channel list.
3. Click **Leave Clan**.
4. Click **Leave Clan** to confirm.

<Image src={computerLeave} alt="The clan menu with Leave Clan at the bottom" />

You can also right-click the clan icon in the clan bar and choose **Leave Clan**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

:::warning
After you leave, you need a new invite to join the clan again, unless the clan is listed on Discover.
:::

The clan owner does not see **Leave Clan**. To leave your own clan, first [transfer the ownership](./create-your-own-clan/transferring-clan-ownership.mdx) to another member.
