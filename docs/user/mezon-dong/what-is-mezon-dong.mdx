# What is Mezon Dong?

Mezon Dong is the token you can send to other people in Mezon. Your balance and every amount are shown in đồng (VND).

## What you can do with Mezon Dong

- **Send it to someone.** Choose a person and an amount, and add a note if you like.
- **Give a coffee.** Send 10,000 đồng to the author of a message from the message menu.
- **See your transactions.** Your transaction history lists what you sent and received.

See [Send Mezon Dong](./send-and-transfer-mezon-dong.mdx) for the steps.

## Where you see your balance

- **Computer:** click your name at the bottom left. The panel shows **Balance**.
- **Phone:** tap **Profile** at the bottom of the screen. Your balance is near **Transfer Funds**.

## How you receive Mezon Dong

People can send you Mezon Dong or give you a coffee. On a phone, you can also show them your transfer QR code. You then get a message from them in a direct message. It shows the amount and the note, for example "Funds Transferred: 10,000₫".

## Names you may see in the app

The app uses more than one name for Mezon Dong. They all mean the same thing:

- **token**, as in **Send Tokens** and **Transfer tokens to another user**.
- **đồng** or **₫**, next to amounts.
- **MMN wallet**, in the desktop app's **Guided tour**.

## Look up transactions

You can look up Mezon Dong transactions in the public Mezon Mainnet Transaction Explorer at [dong.mezon.ai](https://dong.mezon.ai).
