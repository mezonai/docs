# What is Mezon Dong?

Mezon Dong is the token you can send and spend inside Mezon. Your balance and amounts are shown in đồng (₫ / VND).

## What you can do with Mezon Dong

- **Send it to someone:** choose a recipient, enter an amount, and add an optional note.
- **Give a coffee:** send 10,000 đồng to the author of a message from the message menu.
- **Unlock for-sale emojis and stickers:** unlock paid clan emojis or stickers (500 Mezon Dong) on mobile.
- **Upgrade to User Pro or Clan Pro:** pay for annual [User Pro or Clan Pro](../account-and-personalization/pro-plan.mdx) plans from Desktop or Web.
- **See your transactions:** check what you sent and received in your transaction history.

See [Send Mezon Dong](./send-and-transfer-mezon-dong.mdx) for step-by-step instructions.

## Where you see your balance

- **Computer:** click your name at the bottom left. The panel shows **Balance**.
- **Phone:** tap **Profile** at the bottom of the screen. Your balance is near **Transfer Funds**.

## How you receive Mezon Dong

People can send you Mezon Dong or give you a coffee. On a phone, you can also show them your transfer QR code. You then receive a direct message showing the amount and note (for example, `"Funds Transferred: 10,000₫"`).

## Names you may see in the app

The app uses several labels for Mezon Dong that all refer to the same balance:

- **token**, as in **Send Tokens** and **Transfer tokens to another user**
- **đồng** or **₫**, next to amounts
- **MMN wallet**, in the desktop app's **Guided tour**

## Look up transactions

You can look up Mezon Dong transactions on the public Mezon Mainnet Transaction Explorer at [dong.mezon.ai](https://dong.mezon.ai).

:::info Public blockchain visibility
[dong.mezon.ai](https://dong.mezon.ai) is a public explorer: wallet addresses, transaction amounts, timestamps, and blocks can be viewed without logging in. Avoid putting sensitive personal information in transfer notes.
:::
