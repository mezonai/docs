# Send Mezon Dong

Send Mezon Dong to another person, give a coffee for a message, and check what you sent and received.

## Send Mezon Dong to someone

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

<ShotSteps>
<Shot src={phoneProfile} alt="The Profile tab with the balance and Transfer Funds marked">Tap **Profile** at the bottom of the screen. Tap **Transfer Funds**.</Shot>
<Shot src={phoneTransfer} alt="The Transfer Funds form with the person, the amount and a note filled in, Transfer Funds marked">Choose the person. Type the amount. You can add a note. **Android:** tap **Transfer Funds**. **iPhone:** tap **Send**, then tap **Transfer** to confirm.</Shot>
</ShotSteps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click your name at the bottom left.
2. Click **Transfer Funds**.

<Image src={computerPanel} alt="Your profile panel with Balance, Transfer Funds and Transaction History, Transfer Funds marked" />

3. Under **To**, type the person's name and choose them in the list.
4. Under **Amount**, type the amount in VND.
5. You can change the **Note (Optional)**. It already has a default note.
6. Click **Send Tokens**.

<Image src={computerSend} alt="The Send Tokens window with the To, Amount and Note fields, Send Tokens marked" />

You can also start from a direct message with the person:

1. Look at the person's profile on the right. If it is hidden, click **Show User Profile** (the profile icon at the top right).
2. Click **Transfer** (the coin icon) at the top of the profile. The **To** field is already filled in.
3. Type the amount and click **Send Tokens**.

<Image src={computerProfileTransfer} alt="A person's profile in a direct message with the Transfer button marked" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The person gets a message from you in your direct message with them. It shows "Funds Transferred:", the amount and your note.

:::note
The amount must be more than 0 and not more than your balance. You cannot edit a transfer message after it is sent.
:::

## Give a coffee

A coffee sends 10,000 đồng to the author of a message. You cannot give a coffee for your own message.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the message.
2. **Android:** tap **Give Coffee**. **iPhone:** tap **Give A Coffee**.

<Image src={phoneCoffee} alt="The actions of a message with Give Coffee marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the message.
2. Click **Give A Coffee**. The coffee is sent at once, without a confirmation.

<Image src={computerCoffee} alt="The message menu with Give A Coffee marked" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Mezon adds a coffee reaction to the message and sends the author a transfer message for 10,000 đồng.

## Receive Mezon Dong with a QR code

On a phone, you can show a transfer QR code. The person who pays scans it, and Mezon fills in your name as the recipient.

<ShotSteps>
<Shot src={phoneScanner} alt="The QR scanner with My QR Code marked">Tap **Profile**, then the gear icon, then **Scan QR Code** (iPhone: **QR Scan**). Tap **My QR Code**.</Shot>
<Shot src={phoneTransferQr} alt="The Transfer QR tab with a blurred QR code">Tap **Transfer QR** (iPhone: **QR Transfer**). Show this code to the person who pays.</Shot>
</ShotSteps>

The person who pays opens **Scan QR Code** (iPhone: **QR Scan**) the same way and points the camera at your code. Their transfer screen opens with you as the recipient.

## See your transaction history

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Tap **Profile** at the bottom of the screen.
2. **Android:** tap **Transaction History**. **iPhone:** tap **History Transaction**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click your name at the bottom left.
2. Click **Transaction History**.
3. Choose **All Transactions**, **Sent Transactions** or **Received Transactions**.

You can also click **Mezon transfer** under a transfer message to open your history.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
