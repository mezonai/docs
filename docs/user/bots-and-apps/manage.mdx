# Manage bots and apps

A bot is a member of your clan, so you manage it in the clan's member list. An app is a channel, so you manage it like other channels.

## Find the bots in your clan

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan and tap the clan name at the top.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **User Management**, tap **Members**.
4. Tap the bot. The **Edit Member** screen opens.

## Give a bot a role {#give-a-bot-a-role}

A bot can do only what its roles allow, like any member. Create the role first in **Clan Settings** → **Roles**. See [Roles and permissions](../clan/manage/roles-and-permissions.mdx).

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. On the bot's **Edit Member** screen, tap **Edit Roles**.
2. Choose the roles and save.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

You need the **Manage Clan** permission. You can give only roles below your own.

1. On the **Members** page, find the bot.
2. Click **+** in the **ROLES** column next to the bot.
3. Choose the role.

If the list says **No roles available.**, there is no role you can give yet.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Remove a bot from your clan

On a computer you need the **Manage Clan** permission. On a phone you must be the clan owner or have **Administrator**.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. On the bot's **Edit Member** screen, under **Actions**, tap **Kick**.
2. Tap **Kick** again to confirm.

<Tabs groupId="phone-os" queryString>
<TabItem value="android" label="Android">

<Image src={phoneKick} alt="The Edit Member screen of a bot with Kick marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="iphone" label="iPhone">

<Image src={iphoneKick} alt="The Edit Member screen of a bot on iPhone with Kick marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. On the **Members** page, right-click the bot.
2. Click **Remove Member**.

<Image src={computerRemove} alt="The menu of a bot in the member list with Remove Member marked" />

3. Click **Kick** to confirm.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The bot leaves the clan. To use it again later, add it again from the Bots/Apps directory.

:::warning
Do not choose **Transfer Ownership** for a bot. This item gives the whole clan to that member, bot or person. You stop being the clan owner.
:::

## Manage an app

An app lives in its own channel. To rename or remove an app, edit or delete its channel like any other channel.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

### "You are not the owner of this clan"

You see "You are not the owner of this clan. Please choose your own clan." when you add a bot.

This usually means you do not have the **Manage Clan** permission in the clan you chose. Choose another clan, or ask the clan owner to add the bot.

### "You do not have permission to add to this clan"

You see this message when you add an app. You need the **Manage Channels** permission in that clan. Choose another clan, or ask the clan owner to add the app.

### "A channel with this name already exists"

The app needs a new channel, and the category you chose already has a channel with that name. Type another **CHANNEL NAME**, or choose another category, and click **Authorize** again.

### The bot does not answer

1. Look at the clan's member list. If the bot is under offline members, its program is not running. Contact the bot's developer.
2. Check that the bot can see and write in the channel. Its roles need the **View Channels** and **Send Messages** permissions there. See [Give a bot a role](#give-a-bot-a-role).
3. Read the bot's description in the [Bots/Apps directory](https://top.mezon.ai) to check how to use it.
