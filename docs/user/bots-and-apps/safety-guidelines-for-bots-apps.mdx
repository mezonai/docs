# Stay safe with bots and apps

A bot sees and does what its roles allow in your clan. An app learns who you are when you open it. Follow these rules to keep your clan safe.

## Add only bots and apps you trust

Add bots and apps from the [Bots/Apps directory](https://top.mezon.ai) or from developers you know. Do not add a bot from a link you do not trust.

## Know what the permission screen means

The permission screen does not list what the bot can do. A new bot has only the permissions of the @everyone role, which every member has. It gets more only from the roles you give it.

## Give a bot only the roles it needs

- Give the bot a role with only the permissions it needs to work.
- Do not give a bot the **Administrator** permission unless you fully trust its developer. Administrator lets a member manage the entire clan.
- Never choose **Transfer Ownership** for a bot. It gives the whole clan to the bot.

## Remove bots you no longer use

A bot you do not use is still a member of your clan. Remove it. See [Manage bots and apps](./manage-bots-and-apps.mdx).
