# Bots and channel apps

Bots and channel apps bring extra tools into your clan. A bot joins your clan as a member, while a channel app runs as a web app inside its own channel.

## Bots {#bots}

A bot is a program that joins your clan as a member. It can answer messages, post updates or run games, depending on what its developer built.

### What bots are used for

Each bot does what its developer made it do. Some examples:

- Post a reminder in a channel every morning.
- Answer common questions from new members.
- Run a quiz or a game in a channel.
- Post updates from another tool your team uses.

Read a bot's description in the [Bots/Apps directory](https://top.mezon.ai) to learn how to use it.

### How a bot works in your clan

- Only the clan owner or a member with the **Manage Clan** or **Administrator** permission can add a bot. See [Find and add bots and apps](./find-and-add.mdx).
- After it joins, the bot is in the clan's member list. Mezon posts a welcome message for it in the clan.
- Like any member, a bot can do only what its roles allow. You choose its roles. See [Manage bots and apps](./manage.mdx).
- A bot answers only while its developer's program is running. When it is not running, the bot shows as offline in the member list.

### AI agent in voice calls

Mezon has an AI agent, the KOMU Agent, that you can add to a call in a voice channel. Only people who can manage the channel see the button for it.

- **Computer:** the robot button is in the call controls at the bottom of the call. Point at it to see **Add AI agent**. Click it to add the agent. Click it again (**Remove AI agent**) to remove it. Until you close it, a tip called **Turn on KOMU Agent** points at the button.
- **Phone:** the robot button is at the top of the call screen. It has no text. Tap it to add the agent, and tap it again to remove it.

## Channel apps {#apps}

A channel app is a web app that a clan adds as its own channel. When you open it, the app can sign you in with your Mezon account, so you do not need a new account.

### What channel apps are used for

Each app does what its developer made it do. Some examples:

- A shared task board for your team.
- A quiz or a game for clan members.
- A form to collect answers from members.

### How a channel app works in your clan

- Only the clan owner or a member with the **Manage Channels** permission (or **Manage Clan** or **Administrator**) can add an app. See [Find and add bots and apps](./find-and-add.mdx).
- When you add an app, Mezon creates a channel for it in the category you choose.
- On a computer, the clan's apps show as a row of icons in the channel list. Click an icon to open that app in a new browser window.
- When you open an app, Mezon tells the app who you are. This is how the app can sign you in with your Mezon account.

Channel apps are listed alongside bots in the [Bots/Apps directory](https://top.mezon.ai).

## For developers {#for-developers}

Developers can build their own bots and channel apps for Mezon, and connect external services with webhooks:

- **Developer portal:** create a bot or channel app at [mezon.ai/developers](https://mezon.ai/developers/). Click **New Application or Bot**.
- **Developer docs:** start with the [Mezon developer documentation](../../developer/intro.mdx).
- **Bots:** [Create a bot](../../developer/getting-started/create-a-bot.md), then read the [JavaScript/TypeScript bot SDK overview](../../developer/bot-sdks/javascript/overview.md).
- **Channel apps:** [Create a channel app](../../developer/getting-started/create-a-channel-app.md), then read the [Channel Apps overview](../../developer/channel-apps-and-widgets/channel-apps/overview.md).
- **Webhooks:** send messages into Mezon from other services. See the [webhooks overview](../../developer/webhooks/overview.mdx). Clan admins manage webhooks in **Clan Settings** → **Integrations** (see [Integrations](../clan/manage/settings.mdx#integrations)).
