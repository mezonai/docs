# Fix problems with bots and apps

## "You are not the owner of this clan"

You see "You are not the owner of this clan. Please choose your own clan." when you add a bot.

This usually means you do not have the **Manage Clan** permission in the clan you chose. Choose another clan, or ask the clan owner to add the bot.

## "You do not have permission to add to this clan"

You see this message when you add an app. You need the **Manage Channels** permission in that clan. Choose another clan, or ask the clan owner to add the app.

## "A channel with this name already exists"

The app needs a new channel, and the category you chose already has a channel with that name. Type another **CHANNEL NAME**, or choose another category, and click **Authorize** again.

## The bot does not answer

1. Look at the clan's member list. If the bot is under offline members, its program is not running. Contact the bot's developer.
2. Check that the bot can see and write in the channel. Its roles need the **View Channels** and **Send Messages** permissions there. See [Manage bots and apps](./manage-bots-and-apps.mdx).
3. Read the bot's description in the [Bots/Apps directory](https://top.mezon.ai) to check how to use it.
