# Find and add bots and apps

Find bots and apps in the Bots/Apps directory, then add them to a clan where you have the right permission.

## Who can add bots and apps

| To add | You must be |
|---|---|
| A bot | The clan owner, or a member whose role has **Manage Clan** or **Administrator** |
| An app | The clan owner, or a member whose role has **Manage Channels**, **Manage Clan** or **Administrator** |

If you do not have the permission, ask the clan owner to add it for you.

## Find a bot or app

1. Open the Bots/Apps directory: [top.mezon.ai](https://top.mezon.ai).
2. Search or scroll the list. Bots are marked **BOT** and apps are marked **APP**.
3. Click **Invite** next to the one you want.

<Image src={directoryList} alt="The Bots/Apps directory with an Invite button next to each bot and app" />

The add page opens. It works the same for bots and apps, except for the extra fields for apps (see below). If you are not logged in, the page asks you to log in first.

## Add an app to your clan

Adding an app creates a new channel for it. These steps use the add page on a computer.

1. On the add page, click **Add to clan**.
2. Under **ADD TO CLAN**, choose your clan.
3. Under **ADD TO CATEGORY**, choose the category for the new channel.
4. Type a **CHANNEL NAME**, or leave it empty to use the app's name.
5. Click **Authorize**.

The app's channel appears in the category you chose.

## About the permission screen

The screen says that the bot or app "wants to access your Mezon account". It does not list permissions. The screen is in English only.

After you add a bot, it has only the permissions of the @everyone role, which every member has, plus the roles you give it. To change what a bot can do, see [Manage bots and apps](./manage-bots-and-apps.mdx).
