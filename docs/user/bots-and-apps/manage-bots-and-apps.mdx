# Manage bots and apps

A bot is a member of your clan, so you manage it in the clan's member list. An app is a channel, so you manage it like other channels.

## Find the bots in your clan

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the clan and tap the clan name at the top.
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Under **User Management**, tap **Members**.
4. Tap the bot. The **Edit Member** screen opens.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open your clan.
2. Click **Members** at the top of the channel list.

<Image src={computerMembers} alt="The Members page of a clan with a bot in the member list" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Give a bot a role

A bot can do only what its roles allow, like any member. Create the role first in **Clan Settings** → **Roles**. See [Roles and permissions](../clan/create-your-own-clan/manage-clan/overview-settings/roles-and-permissions-management.mdx).

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. On the bot's **Edit Member** screen, tap **Edit Roles**.
2. Choose the roles and save.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

You need the **Manage Clan** permission. You can give only roles below your own.

1. On the **Members** page, find the bot.
2. Click **+** in the **ROLES** column next to the bot.
3. Choose the role.

If the list says **No roles available.**, there is no role you can give yet.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Remove a bot from your clan

On a computer you need the **Manage Clan** permission. On a phone you must be the clan owner or have **Administrator**.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. On the bot's **Edit Member** screen, under **Actions**, tap **Kick**.
2. Tap **Kick** again to confirm.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. On the **Members** page, right-click the bot.
2. Click **Remove Member**.

<Image src={computerRemove} alt="The menu of a bot in the member list with Remove Member marked" />

3. Click **Kick** to confirm.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The bot leaves the clan. To use it again later, add it again from the Bots/Apps directory.

:::warning
Do not choose **Transfer Ownership** for a bot. This item gives the whole clan to that member, bot or person. You stop being the clan owner.
:::

## Manage an app

An app lives in its own channel. To rename or remove an app, edit or delete its channel like any other channel.
