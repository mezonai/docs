# What is a bot?

A bot is a program that joins your clan as a member. It can answer messages, post updates or run games, depending on what its developer built.

## What bots are used for

Each bot does what its developer made it do. Some examples:

- Post a reminder in a channel every morning.
- Answer common questions from new members.
- Run a quiz or a game in a channel.
- Post updates from another tool your team uses.

Read a bot's description in the [Bots/Apps directory](https://top.mezon.ai) to learn how to use it.

## How a bot works in your clan

- Only the clan owner or a member with the **Manage Clan** or **Administrator** permission can add a bot. See [Find and add bots and apps](./explore-and-install-bots-apps.mdx).
- After it joins, the bot is in the clan's member list. Mezon posts a welcome message for it in the clan.
- Like any member, a bot can do only what its roles allow. You choose its roles. See [Manage bots and apps](./manage-bots-and-apps.mdx).
- A bot answers only while its developer's program is running. When it is not running, the bot shows as offline in the member list.

## AI agent in voice calls

Mezon has an AI agent, the KOMU Agent, that you can add to a call in a voice channel. Only people who can manage the channel see the button for it.

- **Computer:** the robot button is in the call controls at the bottom of the call. Point at it to see **Add AI agent**. Click it to add the agent. Click it again (**Remove AI agent**) to remove it. Until you close it, a tip called **Turn on KOMU Agent** points at the button.
- **Phone:** the robot button is at the top of the call screen. It has no text. Tap it to add the agent, and tap it again to remove it.

## Build your own bot

Developers can create bots in the Mezon developer portal. See [Build your own bot or app](./for-developers.mdx).
