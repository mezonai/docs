# What is an app?

An app is a web app that a clan adds as its own channel, called a channel app. When you open it, the app can sign you in with your Mezon account, so you do not need a new account.

## What apps are used for

Each app does what its developer made it do. Some examples:

- A shared task board for your team.
- A quiz or a game for clan members.
- A form to collect answers from members.

## How an app works in your clan

- Only the clan owner or a member with the **Manage Channels** permission (or **Manage Clan** or **Administrator**) can add an app. See [Find and add bots and apps](./explore-and-install-bots-apps.mdx).
- When you add an app, Mezon creates a channel for it in the category you choose.
- On a computer, the clan's apps show as a row of icons in the channel list. Click an icon to open that app in a new browser window.
- When you open an app, Mezon tells the app who you are. This is how the app can sign you in with your Mezon account.

## Find more apps

Apps are listed with bots in the [Bots/Apps directory](https://top.mezon.ai). To build your own app, see [Build your own bot or app](./for-developers.mdx).
