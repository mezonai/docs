# Add friends

Friends are people you know on Mezon. You can message and call a friend at any time, and add friends to group chats. A friend request must be accepted before you become friends.

## Send a friend request

You need the person's Mezon username. On a phone you can also use their phone number. A display name does not work.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the **Messages** tab at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap **Add Friend** at the top.
3. Tap **Add by username or phone number**.
4. Type the username or the phone number.
5. Tap **Send Friend Request**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the Mezon logo at the top left, then click **Friends**.
2. Click **Add Friend** at the top.

<Image src={addFriendsComputer01} alt="The Friends page with the Add Friend button marked" />

3. Type the person's username.
4. Click **Send Friend Request**.

<Image src={addFriendsComputer02} alt="The Add Friend form with the username field and the Send Friend Request button marked" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The person gets your request. You become friends when they accept it.

:::tip
On a computer, your own username is shown under **USERNAME** when you click the gear icon at the bottom left, then **Account**. On a phone, the add-friend screen shows it in the line "By the way, your username is ...".
:::

## Accept or refuse a request

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the **Messages** tab and tap **Add Friend**.
2. Find the request under **Incoming friend request** (Android) or on the **Received** tab (iPhone).
3. Tap the check mark to accept, or the cross to refuse.

You can also open **Profile** → the gear icon (**Settings**) → **Friend Requests**. The **Received** tab lists requests to you. The **Sent** tab lists requests you sent.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. On the **Friends** page, click **Pending**. A red number shows how many requests are waiting.
2. Click the check mark (**Accept**) or the cross (**Reject**).

<Image src={addFriendsComputer03} alt="The Pending tab with the accept and reject buttons marked" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## See your friends

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

Open **Profile** at the bottom, then tap **Your Friends**. Each friend has a message button and a call button.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

The **Friends** page has these tabs:

- **All**: every friend.
- **Online**: friends who are online now.
- **Pending**: friend requests you received or sent. Click ✕ next to a request you sent to cancel it.
- **Block**: people you blocked.

Next to each friend there is a message button and a ⋮ menu with **Start Video Call**, **Start Voice Call**, **Remove Friend** and **Block**.

The right side of the page shows friend activity under **Activity - Work**, **Activity - Live** and **Activity - Play**. Friends appear there when they share their activity from the Mezon desktop app with the **Show My Activity** setting (gear icon → **Activity**).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Remove a friend or block someone {#remove-a-friend-or-block-someone}

You can block anyone, even a person who is not your friend. A blocked person cannot start a new conversation with you.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the **Messages** tab.
2. Press and hold the conversation with the person.
3. Tap **Remove Friend** or **Block User**.

To see or unblock the people you blocked, open **Profile** → the gear icon (**Settings**) → **Account** → **Blocked Users**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. On the **Friends** page, click ⋮ next to the friend.
2. Click **Remove Friend** or **Block**.

To block someone who is not your friend, right-click the conversation with them under **DIRECT MESSAGES** and click **Block**.

To unblock someone, open the **Block** tab on the **Friends** page.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
