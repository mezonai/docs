# Group chats

A group chat is a private conversation with several people. It does not belong to a clan. A group chat can have up to 20 members, you included.

For a bigger team with several channels, roles and voice rooms, create a [clan](../clan/what-is-clan.mdx) instead.

## Create a group chat

You can add only your friends. See [Add friends](./add-friends.mdx).

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the **Messages** tab at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap the round button at the bottom right. On Android, tap **New Group** next.
3. Select two or more friends.
4. Tap **Create**.

You can also start a group from a conversation with one person: tap the person's name at the top, then **Add Members**. Mezon creates a new group with that person and the friends you pick.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the **+** next to **DIRECT MESSAGES**.
2. Select two or more friends. The dialog shows how many more you can add.
3. Click **Create Group Chat**.

<Image src={groupComputer01} alt="The Select Friends dialog with two friends selected and the Create Group Chat button marked" />

You can also open a conversation with one person and click **Add friends to DM** at the top. Mezon creates a new group with that person and the friends you pick.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The new group opens. Its first name is the list of member names.

## Rename the group or change its picture

Every member can do this.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the group chat.
2. Tap the group name at the top.
3. Change the name or the picture. On Android, tap **Customize Group** first.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the group chat.
2. Click the group name at the top, or click **Edit Group**.
3. Type a new **Group Name**, or click the picture to upload a new one.
4. Click **Save**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

A group name can have up to 64 characters.

## Add people

Every member can add their own friends, up to 20 members in total.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the group chat and tap the group name at the top.
2. Tap **Add Members**.
3. Select friends and confirm.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the group chat.
2. Click **Add friends to DM** at the top (the person with a +).
3. Select friends and confirm.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Remove someone from the group

Only the person who created the group can remove members. On a computer, the creator has a crown next to their name in the member list.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

**iPhone**

1. Open the group chat and tap the group name at the top.
2. In **Members**, tap the person.
3. Tap **Remove From Group**, then **Remove**.

**Android:** the app cannot remove members yet. Use an iPhone or a computer.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Open the group chat and click **Show Member List** at the top.
2. Right-click the person.
3. Click **Remove From Group**.

<Image src={groupComputer02} alt="The member menu of a group chat with Remove From Group marked" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Leave a group chat

You can rejoin only if a member adds you again.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the **Messages** tab.
2. Press and hold the group chat.
3. Tap **Leave Group** and confirm.

If you are the last member, the button is **Delete Group**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the group chat under **DIRECT MESSAGES**.
2. Click **Leave Group**.
3. Click **Leave Group** again to confirm.

<Image src={groupComputer03} alt="The menu of a group chat with Leave Group marked" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## What group chats do not have

- No voice or video calls. Calls work only between two people. To talk in a group, use a [voice channel](../clan/create-your-own-clan/manage-clan/clan-structure/channels/voice-channel.mdx) in a clan.
- No threads or topics. These exist only in clan text channels.
