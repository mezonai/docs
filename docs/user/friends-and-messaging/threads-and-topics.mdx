# Threads and topics

Threads and topics keep side conversations out of the main chat of a clan text channel. They do not exist in direct messages or group chats.

## Thread or topic?

| | Thread | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A small channel with a name, under its parent channel | A short discussion about one message |
| Where you see it | In the channel list, under the parent channel | Under the message, as a replies button |
| Who can start one | The clan owner and people whose role has **Manage Threads** | Everyone who can send messages in the channel |
| Private option | Yes: **Private Thread** | No |
| Menu item | **Create Thread** | **Topic Discussion** |

## Start a thread

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the message you want to talk about.
2. Tap **Create Thread**.
3. Type a thread name.
4. Type the first message and send it.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click the message you want to talk about.
2. Click **Create Thread**.

<Image src={threadsComputer01} alt="The message menu in a clan channel with Create Thread marked" />

3. Type a **Thread Name** on the right.
4. To limit who sees it, tick **Private Thread**. Only people you invite and moderators can see a private thread.
5. Type the first message and press **Enter**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The thread appears in the channel list under the parent channel. The channel shows a notice that you started a thread.

A thread name needs at least 4 characters. If **Create Thread** is not in the menu, your role does not have the **Manage Threads** permission.

## Find threads

- **Computer:** click a thread under its channel in the channel list. Or click **Threads** at the top of the channel to see the threads you joined, active threads and archived threads.
- **Phone:** tap the thread in the channel list. Or tap the channel name at the top, then **Threads**.

<Image src={threadsComputer02} alt="The Threads panel of a channel, opened from the Threads button that is marked" />

## Start a topic

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold a message in a clan text channel or thread.
2. Tap **Topic Discussion**.
3. Type your reply and send it.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click a message in a clan text channel or thread.
2. Click **Topic Discussion**.

<Image src={threadsComputer03} alt="The message menu in a clan channel with Topic Discussion marked" />

3. The topic opens on the right. Type your reply and press **Enter**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Under the message, a button shows the number of replies, for example **1 reply**. Click or tap it to open the topic. On phones the button also says **View topic**.

On a computer, a bar under the channel name shows the latest topic or pinned message. Click it to jump there.

<Image src={threadsComputer04} alt="A channel with a topic open on the right and the replies button under the message marked" />

## Find new replies in topics

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

- Open the **Notifications** tab at the bottom, then **Topics** (**Topic** on iPhone). It lists the topics with new activity.
- **Android:** the **View topic** button shows a red number when someone mentions you in that topic.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click **Inbox** at the top right of the channel.
2. Click **Topics**. A red number shows how many topics have unread replies.
3. Click a topic to open it.

<Image src={threadsComputer05} alt="The Inbox with the Topics tab marked and one unread topic" />

A white dot marks a topic with unread replies. A red dot means someone mentioned you there. When someone mentions you in a topic, the channel also shows a **New mentions in topic** button.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
