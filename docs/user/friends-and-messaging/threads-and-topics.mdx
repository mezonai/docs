# Threads and topics

Threads and topics keep side conversations out of the main chat of a clan text channel. They do not exist in direct messages or group chats.

## Thread or topic?

| | Thread | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A small channel with its own name, under its parent channel | A short discussion about one message |
| Starts from | The **Threads** panel, or a message | One message |
| Has a name | Yes | No |
| Private option | Yes: **Private Thread** | No |
| Where you see it | In the channel list, under the parent channel | Under the message as a replies button, or in the Inbox |
| Who can start one | The clan owner and people whose role has **Manage Threads** | Everyone who can send messages in the channel |
| Menu item | **Create Thread** | **Topic Discussion** |

## Threads {#threads}

A thread is a small channel inside a text channel, with its own name. Use a thread for a subject that needs a longer discussion, so the main channel stays easy to follow.

### Start a thread

You need the **Manage Threads** permission. The clan owner always has it. Without it, you do not see **Create** or **Create Thread**.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold the message you want to talk about.
2. Tap **Create Thread**. If you do not see it, swipe the menu up.
3. Type a thread name.
4. To make it private, turn on **Private Thread**.
5. Type the first message and send it.

<Image src={threadsPhone01} alt="The message menu in a clan channel with Create Thread marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

**From a message**

1. Right-click the message you want to talk about.
2. Click **Create Thread**.

<Image src={threadsComputer01} alt="The message menu in a clan channel with Create Thread marked" />

3. Type a **Thread Name** on the right.
4. To limit who sees it, tick **Private Thread**. Only people you invite and moderators can see a private thread.
5. Type the first message and press **Enter**.

**From the Threads panel**

1. Click the **Threads** button at the top right of the channel.
2. Click **Create**.

<Image src={channelThreadsCreate} alt="The Threads panel with the Create button" />

3. Type a name in **Thread Name**, optionally tick **Private Thread**, type the first message and press **Enter**.

<Image src={channelThreadsForm} alt="The New Thread panel with Thread Name, Private Thread and the message box" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The thread appears in the channel list under its parent channel, and the channel shows a notice that you started a thread. A thread name needs at least 4 characters (longer than 3 characters). To invite people to a thread, mention them with **@** inside the thread.

### Find threads

- **Computer:** click a thread under its channel in the channel list. Or click **Threads** at the top of the channel to see the threads you joined, active threads and archived threads.
- **Phone:** tap the thread in the channel list. Or tap the channel name at the top, then tap **Threads**.

<Image src={threadsComputer02} alt="The Threads panel of a channel, opened from the Threads button that is marked" />

Threads with no new messages for a while stop showing in the channel list. The channel's **Hide After Inactivity** setting decides when. You can still find them in the **Threads** list.

## Topics {#topics}

A topic is a short discussion about one message. Replies stay in the topic so the main channel stays easy to follow. A topic has no name and cannot be private.

Anyone who can send messages in a text channel or a thread can start a topic. Topics are not available in direct messages, group chats, voice or stream channels, or inside another topic.

### Start a topic

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Press and hold a message in a clan text channel or thread.
2. Tap **Topic Discussion**. If you do not see it, swipe the menu up.
3. Type your reply and send it.

<Image src={threadsPhone02} alt="The message menu in a clan channel with Topic Discussion marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click a message in a clan text channel or thread.
2. Click **Topic Discussion**.

<Image src={threadsComputer03} alt="The message menu in a clan channel with Topic Discussion marked" />

3. The topic opens on the right. Type your reply and press **Enter**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Under the message, a button shows the number of replies, for example **1 reply** (Android: **1 Reply**). Click or tap it to open the topic. On a phone, the button also says **View topic** (iPhone: **View Topic**).

On a computer, a bar under the channel name shows the latest topic or pinned message. Click it to jump there.

<Image src={threadsComputer04} alt="A channel with a topic open on the right and the replies button under the message marked" />

### Find new replies in topics

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

<ShotSteps plain>
<Shot src={threadsPhone03} alt="The Notifications tab with Topics marked and one topic listed">Open the **Notifications** tab at the bottom, then **Topics** (**Topic** on iPhone). It lists the topics with new activity.</Shot>
<Shot src={threadsPhone04} alt="A message with its replies button and a red number marked">**Android:** the replies button under the message shows a red number when someone mentions you in that topic.</Shot>
</ShotSteps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click **Inbox** at the top right of the channel.
2. Click **Topics**. A red number shows how many topics have unread replies.
3. Click a topic to open it.

<Image src={threadsComputer05} alt="The Inbox with the Topics tab marked and one unread topic" />

A white dot marks a topic with unread replies. A red dot means someone mentioned you there. When someone mentions you in a topic, the channel also shows a **New mentions in topic** button.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
