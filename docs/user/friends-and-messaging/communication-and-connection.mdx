# Communication and connection

Outside a clan, you talk with people in direct messages, group chats and calls. Your messages are the same on your phone, the desktop app and the web app.

## What you can do

- **Add friends** by username. On a phone you can also use a phone number. See [Add friends](./add-friends.mdx).
- **Chat with one person** in a direct message. See [Direct messages](./direct-messages.mdx).
- **Chat with a small group** of up to 20 people. See [Group chats](./group-chats.mdx).
- **Call one person** with voice or video. See [Calls](./calls.mdx).
- **Work with messages**: reply, react, edit, forward, pin, send voice messages and files, and format text. See [Message actions](./message-actions.mdx).
- **Keep side conversations apart** in a clan channel with threads and topics. See [Threads and topics](./threads-and-topics.mdx).

## Who can message you

You decide who can start a new conversation with you:

- Turn on **Don't receive messages from strangers** to allow only friends. See [Choose who can message you](./direct-messages.mdx#choose-who-can-message-you).
- Block a person to stop them from starting a conversation with you. See [Remove a friend or block someone](./add-friends.mdx#remove-a-friend-or-block-someone).

## Limits

| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| Members in a group chat | 20, you included |
| Pinned conversations | 10 on each device |
| People in a call | 2. There are no group calls and no screen sharing in calls. |
| Image in a message (computer and Android apps) | 50 MB |
| Other file in a message (computer and Android apps) | 1 GB |
| Buzz message | 160 characters |

To talk with many people at once or to share your screen, use a [voice channel](../clan/create-your-own-clan/manage-clan/clan-structure/channels/voice-channel.mdx) in a clan.
