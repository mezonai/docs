# Direct messages

A direct message (DM) is a private conversation between you and one other person. It does not belong to a clan. Your conversations are listed under **Messages** on a phone and under **DIRECT MESSAGES** on a computer.

## Start a conversation

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

**With a friend**

1. Open **Profile** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap **Your Friends**.
3. Tap the message button next to your friend.

<Image src={dmPhone01} alt="The Friends list with the message button next to a friend marked" width={270} />

On Android you can also open the **Messages** tab, tap the round button at the bottom right, and pick a friend in the **New Message** list.

**With someone in your clan**

1. In a channel or the member list, tap the person's name or picture.
2. Tap **Message**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

**With a friend**

1. Click the **+** next to **DIRECT MESSAGES** (tooltip **Create DM**).
2. Select one friend.
3. Click **Create DM**.

<Image src={dmComputer01} alt="The Select Friends dialog with one friend selected and the Create DM button marked" />

You can also click the message button next to a friend on the **Friends** page.

**With someone in your clan**

1. In a channel, click the members icon at the top right.
2. Right-click the person and choose **Message**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The conversation opens. Type in the box at the bottom and send.

You cannot start a new conversation with someone who blocked you or whom you blocked. You also cannot start one with a person who turned on **Don't receive messages from strangers**, unless you are friends.

## Choose who can message you {#choose-who-can-message-you}

Turn on **Don't receive messages from strangers** if you want only friends to start conversations with you. Conversations you already have stay open.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open **Profile** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap the gear icon (**Settings**).
3. Tap **Chat**.
4. Turn on **Don't receive messages from strangers**.

<Image src={dmPhone02} alt="The Chat settings with the Don't receive messages from strangers switch marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Click the gear icon next to your name at the bottom left.
2. Click **Chat**. In the desktop app it is under **App Settings**.
3. Turn on **Don't receive messages from strangers**.

<Image src={dmComputer03} alt="The Chat settings with the Don't receive messages from strangers switch marked" />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Pin, mute or close a conversation

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

1. Open the **Messages** tab.
2. Press and hold a conversation.
3. Choose an action:
   - **Pin Conversation**: keep it at the top, under **PINNED**.
   - **Mute Conversation**: stop notifications for it.
   - **Mark as Read**.
   - **Close DM**: hide it from your list.
   - **Block User**.

<Image src={dmPhone03} alt="The menu of a conversation with Pin Conversation marked" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

1. Right-click a conversation under **DIRECT MESSAGES**.
2. Choose an action:
   - **Pin Conversation**: keep it at the top, under **PINNED**.
   - **Mute**: stop notifications for 15 minutes up to **Until I turn it back on**.
   - **Mark as Read**.
   - **Profile**, **Remove Friend** or **Block**.

<Image src={dmComputer02} alt="The menu of a direct message with Pin Conversation marked" />

To close a conversation, point at it and click the ✕ next to its name. Then click **Close Direct Message**.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

You can pin up to 10 conversations. Pins are saved on each device separately.

Closing a conversation only hides it from your list. To open it again, start a new conversation with the same person.

## Find photos and files in a conversation

On a computer, the top of a direct message has **Gallery** (photos and videos) and **Files** buttons. Use **Pinned Messages** to see the messages you pinned there. See [Pinned messages](../clan/create-your-own-clan/manage-clan/clan-structure/channels/text-channel/pinned-messages.mdx).
