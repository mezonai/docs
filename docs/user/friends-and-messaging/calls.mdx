# Calls

You can make a voice or video call to one other person. Calls are only between two people. Group chats have no call button, and you cannot share your screen in a call.

To talk with several people, or to share your screen, use a [voice channel](../clan/channels/voice-channel.mdx) in a clan.

## Start a call

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

Start a call in one of these places:

- In a conversation with the person: tap the phone icon (voice call) or the camera icon (video call) at the top.
- On the person's profile: tap **Call**.
- In **Profile** → **Your Friends**: tap the call button next to the friend.

<Image src={callsPhone01} alt="The top of a direct message with the phone icon marked" width={270} />

A voice call can become a video call: turn on your camera in the call.

The first time, allow Mezon to use the microphone. On Android 14 or later, Mezon may also ask you to turn on **Fullscreen incoming calls**, so calls ring while the phone is locked.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

Start a call in one of these places:

- In a conversation with the person: click the phone icon (**Start voice call**) or the camera icon (**Start Video Call**) at the top.
- On the **Friends** page: click ⋮ next to the friend, then **Start Voice Call** or **Start Video Call**.
- On the person's profile card: click the call button (**Call**).

<Image src={callsComputer01} alt="The top of a direct message with the voice call and video call buttons marked" />

<Image src={callsComputer02} alt="The friend menu on the Friends page with Start Video Call and Start Voice Call marked" />

The first time, allow Mezon to use the microphone and the camera.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

The conversation shows **Started voice call** or **Started video call**. If you are already in a call, Mezon says **You are on another call**.

## Answer a call

A call rings on every phone and computer where you are logged in. Answer it on one of them.

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

The phone shows an incoming call screen with the caller's name. Answer or decline it there. On iPhone it looks like a normal phone call.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

A card opens with the caller's name. Use its buttons to answer with voice, answer with video, or decline.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## During a call

<Tabs groupId="platform" queryString>
<TabItem value="phone" label="Phone">

- Microphone button: turn your microphone off or on.
- **Speaker**: switch between the earpiece and the loudspeaker.
- Camera button: turn your camera on or off. You can also switch between the front and back camera.
- Red button: end the call.

On both Android and iPhone, you can minimize an ongoing 1:1 call (Android: tap **Minimize call**) and keep using the app. Tap the **Ongoing call** banner at the top of the screen to return to the call.

<Image src={callsPhone02} alt="A voice call in progress with the speaker, end call and microphone buttons at the bottom" width={270} />

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="computer" label="Computer">

- Camera button: turn your camera on or off.
- Microphone button: turn your microphone off or on.
- Settings button: choose your microphone and speaker.
- Red button: end the call.

In the web app, click a video to make it bigger.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
