# Frequently asked questions

## Getting started

### What is Mezon?

Mezon is an app to chat, call, stream, and work together. You talk with people one to one, in group chats, and in communities called clans. See [Welcome to Mezon](../welcome.mdx).

### Which devices can I use?

Android phones (Android 7.0 or later), iPhones and iPads (iOS 13.0 or later), Windows computers (Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64-bit), Macs (macOS 13.0 or later), Linux computers (64-bit Debian or Ubuntu), and any computer with a web browser. See [Download and install Mezon](../account-and-personalization/download-and-install.mdx).

### How do I create an account?

Log in with a code sent to your email or by SMS to your mobile number. The apps accept Vietnamese mobile numbers (+84). Your first login creates your account. See [Sign up and log in](../account-and-personalization/sign-up-and-log-in.mdx).

### I did not get the code. What can I do?

Wait until the timer ends, then select **Resend OTP**. Check that you typed your email or phone number correctly. If you are outside Vietnam, log in with your email.

### Can I use Mezon on several devices at the same time?

Yes. To see where you are logged in, or to log out one device, open **Settings → Devices** on a computer or on Android. See [Profile and settings](../account-and-personalization/profile-and-settings.mdx#devices).

### Can I log in on a computer with my phone?

Yes. Scan the QR code on the computer's login screen with the Mezon app on your phone. See [Log in on a computer with your phone](../account-and-personalization/sign-up-and-log-in.mdx#log-in-on-a-computer-with-your-phone-qr-code).

## Clans

### What is a clan?

A clan is a group in Mezon, for a team, a class, or a community. A clan has text channels for messages, voice channels for meetings, and stream channels for live broadcasts. See [What is a clan](../clan/what-is-clan.mdx).

### How do I join a clan?

Open an invite link or scan an invite QR code from someone in the clan, or find a public clan with **Discover**. See [Join and explore clans](../clan/join-and-explore-clans.mdx).

### How many clans can I join?

Up to 50. This includes the clans you created.

### Who can invite people to a clan, and do invite links expire?

Any clan member can share an invite link or QR code. Anyone with the link can join, so share it only with people you want in the clan. See [Invite people to your clan](../clan/invite-people.mdx).

### Can I create my own clan?

Yes. Select **+** in the clan list. See [Create a clan](../clan/create-a-clan.mdx).

### How do I choose which clan notifications I get?

On a computer, right-click the clan icon and choose **Notification Settings**. Then choose **All messages**, **Only @mention**, or **Nothing**. See [Notifications](../clan/manage/notifications.mdx).

## Calls, Go Live, and bots

### Can I share my screen?

Yes, in a voice channel, on a computer and on a phone. On a phone, only people who joined as a speaker can share. See [Voice channels](../clan/channels/voice-channel.mdx).

### Can I Go Live in a stream channel?

Yes. On a computer (Desktop or Web), any member of a stream channel can click **Go Live** when nobody else is broadcasting, or channel managers can connect OBS Studio 30+ using WHIP. Everyone on phone and computer can watch. See [Stream channels](../clan/channels/stream-channel.mdx).

### Other people cannot hear me. What can I do?

Check that Mezon has microphone permission. On a computer, open **Settings → Voice** to choose and test the right microphone. In a browser, allow the microphone when prompted.

### Can I add bots to my clan?

Yes. Find bots and apps at [top.mezon.ai](https://top.mezon.ai/) and click **Invite**. You must be the clan owner, or have **Manage Clan** for a bot or **Manage Channels** for an app. See [Find and add bots and apps](../bots-and-apps/find-and-add.mdx).

## Pro plans and Mezon Dong

### What is the difference between User Pro and Clan Pro?

**User Pro** (500,000 Mezon Dong / year) upgrades your personal account (larger external calls, Pro badge, avatar frames, higher limits). **Clan Pro** (2,000,000 Mezon Dong / year, purchased by the clan owner) upgrades one specific clan (larger SFU calls, external events, livestream, storage, sound effects, and clan Pro badge/frame). Creating a clan from a User Pro account creates a normal clan. See [User Pro and Clan Pro](../account-and-personalization/pro-plan.mdx).

### What is Mezon Dong, and are transactions public?

Mezon Dong is the token used inside Mezon for transfers, coffee tips, unlocking paid stickers/emojis, and upgrading to Pro plans. Transactions can be looked up publicly at [dong.mezon.ai](https://dong.mezon.ai) without logging in. See [What is Mezon Dong](../mezon-dong/what-is-mezon-dong.mdx).

### How do I send Mezon Dong?

On a computer, click your name at the bottom left, then **Transfer Funds**. On a phone, open **Profile**, then **Transfer Funds**. See [Send Mezon Dong](../mezon-dong/send-and-transfer-mezon-dong.mdx).
