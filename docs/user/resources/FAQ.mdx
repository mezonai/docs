# Frequently asked questions

## Getting started

### What is Mezon?

Mezon is an app to chat, call and work together. You talk with people one to one, in group chats, and in communities called clans. See [Welcome to Mezon](../welcome.mdx).

### Which devices can I use?

Android phones (Android 7.0 or later), iPhones and iPads (iOS 13.0 or later), Windows computers (Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64-bit), Macs (macOS 13.0 or later), Linux computers (64-bit Debian or Ubuntu), and any computer with a web browser. See [Download and install Mezon](../account-and-personalization/download-and-settings.mdx).

### How do I create an account?

Log in with a code sent to your email or by SMS to your mobile number. The apps accept Vietnamese mobile numbers (+84). Your first login creates your account. See [Sign up and log in](../account-and-personalization/sign-up-and-log-in.mdx).

### I did not get the code. What can I do?

Wait until the timer ends, then select **Resend OTP**. Check that you typed your email or phone number correctly. If you are outside Vietnam, log in with your email.

### Can I use Mezon on several devices at the same time?

Yes. To see where you are logged in, or to log out one device, open **Settings → Devices** on a computer or on Android. See [Profile and settings](../account-and-personalization/personalization-settings.mdx#devices).

### Can I log in on a computer with my phone?

Yes. Scan the QR code on the computer's login screen with the Mezon app on your phone. See [Log in on a computer with your phone](../account-and-personalization/sign-up-and-log-in.mdx#log-in-on-a-computer-with-your-phone-qr-code).

## Clans

### What is a clan?

A clan is a group in Mezon, for a team, a class or a community. A clan has text channels for messages and voice channels for talking. See [What is a clan](../clan/what-is-clan.mdx).

### How do I join a clan?

Open an invite link from someone in the clan, or find a public clan with **Discover**. See [Join and explore clans](../clan/join-and-explore-clans.mdx).

### How many clans can I join?

Up to 50. This includes the clans you created.

### How long does an invite link work?

One day after it was made, or seven days for a community clan. Anyone with the link can join while it works. If a link has expired, ask for a new one.

### Can I create my own clan?

Yes. Select **+** in the clan list. See [Create a clan](../clan/create-your-own-clan/create-a-clan.mdx).

### How do I choose which clan notifications I get?

On a computer, right-click the clan icon and choose **Notification Settings**. Then choose **All messages**, **Only @mention** or **Nothing**. See [Notifications in a clan](../clan/create-your-own-clan/manage-clan/managing-notifications-in-a-clan.mdx).

## Calls, files and bots

### Can I share my screen?

Yes, in a voice channel, on a computer and on a phone. On a phone, only people who joined as a speaker can share. See [Voice channels](../clan/create-your-own-clan/manage-clan/clan-structure/channels/voice-channel.mdx).

### Other people cannot hear me. What can I do?

Check that Mezon may use your microphone. On a computer, open **Settings → Voice** to choose the right microphone and to test it. In a browser, allow the microphone when the browser asks.

### Can I add bots to my clan?

Yes. Find bots and apps at [top.mezon.ai](https://top.mezon.ai/) and click **Invite**. You must be the clan owner, or have the **Manage Clan** permission for a bot or **Manage Channels** for an app. See [Explore and install bots and apps](../bots-and-apps/explore-and-install-bots-apps.mdx).

## Mezon Dong

### What is Mezon Dong?

Mezon Dong is the currency you send to other people inside Mezon. The app shows amounts in đồng. See [What is Mezon Dong](../mezon-dong/what-is-mezon-dong.mdx).

### How do I send Mezon Dong?

On a computer, click your name at the bottom left, then **Transfer Funds**. On a phone, open **Profile**, then **Transfer Funds**. See [Send Mezon Dong](../mezon-dong/send-and-transfer-mezon-dong.mdx).
